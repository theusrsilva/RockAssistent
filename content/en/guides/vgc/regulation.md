The regulation says who's allowed, which items are legal and for how long. It changes a few times a year; the date it
was checked is in the box below.

:::vgc-regulation

## In practice

- **Bring 6, pick 4**: the opponent sees your 6 before choosing. Build a team where different groups of 4 work.
- **Mega Evolution**: you may have more than one Pokémon holding a Mega Stone, but only one evolves per battle.
- **No Terastallization**: types don't change mid-battle (only through Mega).
- **Closed list**: only Pokémon on the regulation's roster can enter; everything else is out.
- **The game is the source**: the full list is in Champions, under Recruit → Roster Info. New Pokémon and items
  arrive with each regulation.
- **Champions values**: some moves have different power or PP than in the main games. Older calculators can be off.

## Who shows up the most now

- Most used in M-C tournaments: Rillaboom, Sneasler, Incineroar, Garchomp, Farigiraf, Charizard (Mega Y), Archaludon,
  Golisopod (Mega), Kingambit and Indeedee.
- Common pairs: Rillaboom + Incineroar, Pelipper + Archaludon, Kingambit + Sneasler.
