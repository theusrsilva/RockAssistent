Teams written by RockAssistent for the current regulation. They aren't copies of players' teams: they're the format's
most common archetypes, built with Pokémon that are strong right now. Each has the full paste, the idea, how to play
and what to watch out for.

:::vgc-teams

## What you can do on the teams page

- **Copy paste**: paste it into Showdown or a team builder.
- **Analyze**: weaknesses by type, move coverage and speeds.
- **Import to Boxes**: creates the 6 in Scarlet/Violet and puts them in the vault inbox. Mega Stones don't exist in SV,
  so whoever holds one comes without an item.

## The archetypes

| Archetype | Plan | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rain | Pelipper sets rain; Water moves and Swift Swim get stronger | Pelipper + Archaludon |
| Sun | Mega Charizard Y sets sun; Fire moves and Chlorophyll get stronger | Charizard + Venusaur |
| Sand | Mega Tyranitar sets sand; Sand Rush doubles Speed | Tyranitar + Excadrill |
| Trick Room | Slow Pokémon move first for 4 turns | Farigiraf + Indeedee |
| Balance | Fake Out, Intimidate and switching; no single plan | Rillaboom + Incineroar |
| Hyper offense | Tailwind and maximum damage in the first turns | Salamence + Sneasler |
| Screens and setup | Reflect + Light Screen and a Pokémon that boosts itself | Grimmsnarl + Volcarona |

## How to choose

- **First team**: Rillaboom + Incineroar (balance). It forgives mistakes and teaches Fake Out and switching.
- **You like a clear plan**: rain or Trick Room. The first turn is almost always the same.
- **You like attacking**: Tailwind (hyper offense) or sun.
