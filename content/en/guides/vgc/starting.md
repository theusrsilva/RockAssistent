## What is VGC

- VGC (Video Game Championships): the official Pokémon tournament circuit, with Regionals, Internationals and Worlds.
- In the 2026 season VGC moved to **Pokémon Champions** (Switch and mobile). The rules for each period come in a
  **regulation** (Regulation Set M-A, M-B, M-C...). The current one is in [Current regulation](/guias/vgc/champions/regulation).
- Champions' own Ranked Battles use the same regulation: you can practice at home.

## What a battle looks like

- **Double** battles: two Pokémon on each side at the same time.
- Team of **6**; before each battle both players see the other 6 and pick **4**.
- Everyone fights at **level 50**, even if they are at another level.
- No repeated species and no repeated items on the team.
- One Mega Evolution per battle (only for Pokémon that have one). There is no Terastallization in Champions.
- Clock: 7 minutes per player, 45 s per turn.

## What changes from the regular game

- **Moves that hit both opponents** (Heat Wave, Rock Slide, Earthquake) do 75% damage, but they're very valuable.
- **Earthquake also hits your partner**: pair it with a Flying partner, a Levitate user or Protect.
- **Protect** on almost every team: a protected turn makes the opponent waste an attack, Tailwind or Trick Room.
- **Speed rules**: Tailwind (doubles your side's Speed for 4 turns) and Trick Room (slow Pokémon move first for 4
  turns) decide many games.
- **Support moves** that barely matter in the story are strong here: Fake Out, Follow Me, Rage Powder, Helping Hand,
  Wide Guard, Parting Shot.
- **Intimidate** lowers both opponents' Attack on entry: that's why Incineroar is on so many teams.

## Stat Points instead of EVs

- Champions replaces EVs and IVs with **Stat Points (SP)**: 66 points per Pokémon, at most 32 in one stat.
- Each SP adds 1 point to the stat at level 50. 32 SP ≈ 252 EVs the old way (1 SP ≈ 8 EVs).
- There are no IVs: every Pokémon has the maximum. Natures still apply (+10% to one stat, −10% to another).
- Adding SP costs Victory Points (VP): 5 VP per point. Removing them is free.
- More in [Reading a paste](/guias/vgc/champions/reading-a-team).

## How to start

1. Read the [current regulation](/guias/vgc/champions/regulation) to know who's allowed.
2. Pick one of the [sample teams](/guias/vgc/times) and copy the paste.
3. Learn the team's first turns (each team's "how to play") and play Ranked Battles.
4. Swap one Pokémon at a time when something bothers you and check the effect in [My team](/guias/vgc/minha-equipe).
5. Watch tournament matches: the commentators explain why each play was made.

## Quick tips

- Think about the 4 you bring against the team you're looking at, not just your lead.
- Count how many turns of Tailwind or Trick Room are left.
- Protect the Pokémon the opponent wants to KO; attack the one they want to protect.
- A common team played well beats a "genius" team played badly.
