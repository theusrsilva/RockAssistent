## The basics

- VGC (Video Game Championships): the official Pokémon tournament circuit.
- Double battles: two Pokémon on each side at once.
- Team of 6, bring 4 to each battle (the opponent sees all 6 first).
- Everyone battles at level 50.
- No repeated species and no repeated items on a team.
- Which Pokémon and mechanics are allowed changes with each season's regulation.
