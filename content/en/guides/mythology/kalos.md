## The region

- Based on northern France (Lumiose City ≈ Paris).
- Games: X/Y. Legends: Z-A returns to Lumiose City (see [Lumiose](/guias/mythology/lumiose/lumiose)).
- Core lore: an ancient war, the ultimate weapon and Mega Evolution.

## The war of 3,000 years ago

- **AZ**, a 3 m tall king of Kalos, had a Floette he loved.
- The Floette was taken to fight in a war and died.
- AZ built a machine to bring it back (using the life of other Pokémon); the Floette came back and got the
  Eternal Flower.
- Angry at the war, AZ turned the machine into the **ultimate weapon** and ended the conflict, killing both sides.
- The Floette left him when it found out; AZ became immortal and wandered for 3,000 years looking for it.
- The ultimate weapon is hidden under Geosenge Town, the town of standing stones; the graves of the Pokémon that powered it line Route 10, whose stones absorbed their energy.

## Mega Evolution

- Mega Stones and Key Stones come from the same phenomenon as the ultimate weapon (Prof. Sycamore's theory).
- Shalour City and the Tower of Mastery keep the tradition; Korrina and her grandfather hand over the Mega Ring.
- The Tower of Mastery has a statue of a Mega Evolved Lucario; Korrina battles with Mega Lucario.

## Trio

- **Aura trio:** Xerneas, Yveltal and Zygarde (life, destruction and balance).
- Zygarde got Cells, Cores and the 10%, 50% and Complete forms in SM/USUM.

## Real-world parallels

- Kalos = France; Lumiose = Paris; Prism Tower = Eiffel Tower.
- Parfum Palace = Palace of Versailles.
- Tower of Mastery in Shalour = Mont-Saint-Michel.
- Geosenge Town's stones = the Carnac alignments (Brittany).
- The name "Kalos" comes from the Greek for "beauty".
