## The region

- Hisui is the Sinnoh of long ago, when the first settlers arrived (Legends: Arceus).
- Based on Hokkaidō in the Meiji era, when settlers from the south moved to the island.
- Areas: Obsidian Fieldlands, Crimson Mirelands, Cobalt Coastlands, Coronet Highlands, Alabaster Icelands.

## The clans and "Sinnoh"

- **Diamond Clan** (leader Adaman): worships the god of time, which it calls "Sinnoh" (Dialga).
- **Pearl Clan** (leader Irida): worships the god of space, which it also calls "Sinnoh" (Palkia).
- The two clans fight over this; the player helps unite them.
- **Galaxy Expedition Team** (Jubilife Village, Commander Kamado): research, the Pokédex and settlement.
- A rift in the sky (space-time rift) over Mt. Coronet brings the player and sends the Noble Pokémon into a frenzy.

## Trios and quartets

- **Creation trio:** Dialga, Palkia, Giratina (Origin Formes debut here for Dialga and Palkia).
- **Lake guardians:** Uxie, Mesprit, Azelf.
- **Forces of Nature:** Tornadus, Thundurus, Landorus + **Enamorus** (debuts in Hisui; the quartet is complete).

## Antagonists

| Who | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Volo (Ginkgo Guild) | Find Arceus and remake the world; uses Giratina |
| Kamado | Distrusts the player for falling from the sky; banishes the player for a while |

- **Cogita:** a mysterious woman who knows the myths; she and Volo look like Cynthia, but no family tie was ever confirmed.

## Real-world parallels

- Hisui = Hokkaidō before it got that name (old name Ezo); the clans recall the island's native people (Ainu).
- Jubilife Village recalls Meiji-era settler villages.
