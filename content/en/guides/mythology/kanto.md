## The region

- Based on the Kantō region of Japan (Saffron City ≈ Tokyo).
- Games: Red/Blue/Yellow, FireRed/LeafGreen, Let's Go Pikachu/Eevee; also the postgame of GSC and HGSS.
- No creation myth of its own: the focus is Mew, Mewtwo and the three birds.

## Legends and lore

- **Mew:** named by the Pokémon Mansion journals as the ancestor of all Pokémon; seen in the jungles of Guyana.
- **Mewtwo:** cloned from Mew's DNA on Cinnabar Island; the experiment got out of control and destroyed the
  lab.
- **Legendary birds:** Articuno (ice), Zapdos (thunder) and Moltres (fire); each one in a remote spot on the map.
- **Pokémon Tower (Lavender Town):** a Pokémon graveyard; the ghost of Cubone's mother, killed by Team Rocket.
- **Mt. Moon:** Clefairy and the Moon Stone, tied to the idea that they came from space.
- **Fossils:** Omanyte, Kabuto and Aerodactyl (Old Amber), revived at the Cinnabar lab.
- **Sevii Islands (FRLG):** Tanoby Ruins (Unown), Birth Island (Deoxys) and Navel Rock (Lugia and Ho-Oh).

## Trios

- **Legendary birds:** Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres. Galarian forms in SwSh (The Crown Tundra DLC).
- **Kanto fossils:** Omanyte, Kabuto and Aerodactyl (one fossil at a time in RBY and FRLG: Helix or Dome).

## Villainous team

| Team | Leader | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team Rocket | Giovanni (also the Viridian Gym Leader) | Profit from Pokémon: Game Corner, stealing Silph Co.'s Master Ball, rare Pokémon |

- Places: Rocket Hideout (Celadon Game Corner), Pokémon Tower, Silph Co. (Saffron City).
- FRLG: Team Rocket carries on in the Sevii Islands (Rocket Warehouse, Five Island).
- After his defeat at the Viridian Gym, Giovanni disbands Team Rocket and disappears.

## Real-world parallels

- Kanto = Kantō (Japan), with the map in the same general shape.
- Lavender Town recalls Japanese temple graveyards.
- Celadon Department Store recalls Tokyo's department stores.
