## The city

- Legends: Z-A takes place only in Lumiose City (Kalos), after the events of X/Y, during a plan to rebuild the city.
- Based on Paris; the Prism Tower (≈ Eiffel Tower) stands in the center, in Centrico Plaza.
- Districts: Vert, Bleu, Magenta, Rouge and Jaune, with sectors, avenues and the Saison Canal.
- Mega Dimension DLC: Hyperspace Lumiose, a distorted version of the city.

## The redevelopment plan

- **Quasartico Inc.** (president Jacinthe) runs Lumiose's redevelopment project.
- **Wild Zones:** fenced areas where wild Pokémon live inside the city (20 in the base game).
- **Z-A Royale:** nightly tournament by ranks (Z → A) in the Battle Zones; promotion through Ticket Points.
- Postgame: Infinite Z-A Royale at Quasartico Inc.

## Mega Evolution and Rogue Megas

- Mega Evolution is the game's battle mechanic (Mega Ring), in real-time battles.
- **Rogue Mega Evolutions:** Pokémon that Mega Evolve on their own and run wild through the city.
- The player calms each one and gets its Mega Stone.
- The link to the Kalos legend: AZ's Floette and the Eternal Flower, and Zygarde guarding the balance.

## Lore tied to X/Y

- **AZ** runs Hotel Z, base of the player and the team (Team MZ).
- **Lysandre** shows up as "L"; Team Flare left its marks (Lysandre Café, Lysandre Labs).
- The climax is the Hyperrogue Ange Floette (AZ's Floette, with the Eternal Flower), with Complete Zygarde and Mega Zygarde.
- The Looker Bureau (Looker and Emma) is open again.

## Groups

- **Aura trio:** Xerneas, Yveltal, Zygarde (all three show up in the city).
- **Weather trio:** Groudon, Kyogre, Rayquaza (DLC).

- Lumiose = Paris; Prism Tower = Eiffel Tower; avenues that radiate from the center.
- French names: districts (Vert, Bleu, Rouge, Jaune), Gare de Lumiose, Hotel Richissime.
