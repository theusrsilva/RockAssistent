## The region

- Based on Hokkaidō, Japan (cold in the north, Mt. Coronet splitting the region in half).
- Games: Diamond/Pearl/Platinum and Brilliant Diamond/Shining Pearl. Hisui (Legends: Arceus) is ancient Sinnoh.
- It has the most complete creation myth in the series.

## The creation myth

- At first there was only chaos; an egg came from it, and from the egg hatched **Arceus** (the "Original One").
- Arceus created **Dialga** (time) and **Palkia** (space), and with them the universe.
- It also created **Uxie**, **Mesprit** and **Azelf**, who gave people knowledge, emotion and willpower.
- **Giratina** was banished to the Distortion World (the reverse world) for its violence; it stands for antimatter.
- Spear Pillar, on top of Mt. Coronet, is the place tied to creation.
- The books at the Canalave Library and the ruins of Celestic Town keep these stories.
- An odd myth from the library: long ago Pokémon and people married each other.

## Other legends

- **Regigigas:** said to have pulled the continents with ropes; sleeps in the Snowpoint Temple and made the other Regis.
- **Cresselia and Darkrai:** good dreams (Lunar Wing) against nightmares; neighboring islands, Fullmoon and Newmoon.
- **Heatran:** lives in the Stark Mountain volcano; the Magma Stone keeps it there.
- **Manaphy:** the "prince of the sea"; Phione hatches from Manaphy with Ditto.
- **Shaymin:** spreads flowers; Gracidea turns it into Sky Forme.

## Trios

- **Creation trio:** Dialga, Palkia and Giratina.
- **Lake guardians:** Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf.
- **Lunar duo:** Cresselia and Darkrai.

## Villainous team

| Team | Leader | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team Galactic | Cyrus | Destroy the current world and create a new one without "spirit" (emotions) |

- Plan: capture the lake guardians, make the Red Chain and control Dialga/Palkia at Spear Pillar.
- Platinum: Giratina steps in and takes Cyrus to the Distortion World.
- Commanders: Mars, Jupiter, Saturn; Charon (Platinum).

## Real-world parallels

- Sinnoh = Hokkaidō; Snowpoint recalls the island's snowy north.
- Pastoria (Great Marsh) recalls the Kushiro marsh.
