## The region

- Based on the Iberian Peninsula (Spain and Portugal); the Naranja/Uva Academy is in Mesagoza, in the center.
- Games: Scarlet/Violet, with the DLCs The Teal Mask (Kitakami) and The Indigo Disk (Blueberry Academy).
- Core lore: the Great Crater of Paldea (Area Zero), Terastal and the Paradox Pokémon.

## Area Zero and Terastal

- The crater in the middle of the region is full of Tera crystals; their energy causes the Terastal phenomenon.
- The Professor (Sada in Scarlet, Turo in Violet) built a time machine in the Zero Lab.
- The machine brought Paradox Pokémon from the past (Scarlet) or the future (Violet).
- The in-game Professor is an AI; the real one died protecting the research.
- The "Scarlet Book" / "Violet Book" is the expedition book that inspired the Professor.

## Treasures of Ruin

- Local legend: an ancient king of Paldea had four treasures (scrolls, sword, vessels and beads).
- People's grudges and envy turned the treasures into the four Pokémon of ruin.
- Each one was sealed in a shrine with 8 stakes spread across the region.

| Chi-Yu | Beads | Firescourge Shrine (above Fury Falls) | Blue |

## Kitakami (The Teal Mask)

- The local legend says an ogre attacked the village and three heroes (Okidogi, Munkidori, Fezandipiti) drove it away.
- The truth: the "ogre" was **Ogerpon**, defending its human partner; the three were the villains.
- Ogerpon's masks (Teal, Wellspring, Hearthflame, Cornerstone) change its type and Tera.
- Bloodmoon Ursaluna shows up on foggy nights in the Timeless Woods.

## Groups

- **Ruinous quartet:** Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Ting-Lu, Chi-Yu.
- **Loyal Three:** Okidogi, Munkidori, Fezandipiti (tied to Pecharunt).
- **Paradox:** ancient and future versions of known Pokémon (e.g. Great Tusk ↔ Iron Treads; Walking Wake and Iron
  Leaves recall Suicune and Virizion; Gouging Fire/Raging Bolt and Iron Boulder/Iron Crown, Entei/Raikou and Terrakion/Cobalion).

## Real-world parallels

- Paldea = Iberian Peninsula; the Academy recalls the University of Salamanca.
- Kitakami recalls rural Japan (festival, rice fields).
- Blueberry Academy is in Unova, in a dome under the sea.
