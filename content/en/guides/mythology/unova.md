## The region

- Based on the New York area (Castelia City ≈ Manhattan) and the northeastern US.
- Games: Black/White and Black 2/White 2 (two years later).
- First region far from Japan; theme of truth versus ideals.

## The legend of the two heroes

- A single dragon helped the twin brothers who founded Unova.
- The brothers quarreled (one sought truth, the other ideals) and the dragon split in two:
  - **Reshiram**, the white dragon of truth, with the older brother;
  - **Zekrom**, the black dragon of ideals, with the younger one.
- The war between them nearly destroyed the region; the dragons turned into stones (Light Stone and Dark Stone).
- **Kyurem** is said to be the "empty shell" left from the original dragon; it lives in the Giant Chasm.
- Dragonspiral Tower is Unova's oldest building, tied to the dragons.

## Other legends

- **Forces of Nature:** Tornadus (wind) and Thundurus (lightning) wreaked havoc; Landorus (land, fertility) calmed
  them. The Abundant Shrine is dedicated to it.
- **Swords of Justice:** Cobalion, Terrakion and Virizion protected Pokémon from human wars; Keldeo is the young one.
- **Victini:** brings victory to whoever has it; lives in the Liberty Garden.
- **Meloetta:** the ancient song (Relic Song) changes its form.
- **Genesect:** a 300-million-year-old fossil modified by Team Plasma, with a cannon on its back.
- **Volcarona:** called a "sun" when volcanic ash covered the sky; Relic Castle.

## Trios and quartets

- **Tao trio:** Reshiram, Zekrom, Kyurem.
- **Forces of Nature (Kami trio):** Tornadus, Thundurus, Landorus (Enamorus joins in Hisui).
- **Swords of Justice:** Cobalion, Terrakion, Virizion (+ Keldeo).

| Team | Leader | Goal |

## Real-world parallels

- Unova = New York area; Castelia = Manhattan; Skyarrow Bridge recalls the city's bridges.
- Liberty Garden recalls Liberty Island (Statue of Liberty).
