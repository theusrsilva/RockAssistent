## The region

- Based on Kyūshū, Japan (the map is the island rotated), with many islands and lots of sea.
- Games: Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald and Omega Ruby/Alpha Sapphire.
- Core lore: the clash between land and sea, calmed by the sky.

## The weather myth

- Groudon raised the continents; Kyogre filled the seas.
- The two fought until Rayquaza came down from the sky and calmed them; then they slept (Groudon in magma, Kyogre at the bottom of the sea).
- The Red Orb and Blue Orb, kept on Mt. Pyre, control the two.
- ORAS: the orbs trigger Primal Reversion (an ancient, stronger form).
- Sootopolis City guards the Cave of Origin, where Pokémon souls return; the Sky Pillar is Rayquaza's shrine.

## Other legends

- **Regis:** Regirock, Regice and Registeel were sealed away by ancient people; the Sealed Chamber (Braille) opens the three
  tombs. Linked to Regigigas (Sinnoh).
- **Latios and Latias:** dragons that understand people; Southern Island keeps the Soul Dew.
- **Jirachi:** wakes for 7 days every 1,000 years, when the Millennium Comet passes, and grants wishes.
- **Deoxys:** born from a virus that came from space on a meteorite; the Mossdeep Space Center watches meteors.
- **Draconid people (ORAS):** an ancient people who made the pact with Rayquaza; Zinnia is the Lorekeeper.
- **Mt. Chimney:** the meteorite used by Team Magma.

## Trios

- **Weather trio:** Groudon, Kyogre, Rayquaza.
- **Legendary titans (Regis):** Regirock, Regice, Registeel (+ Regigigas as leader; Regieleki and Regidrago in SwSh).
- **Eon duo:** Latias and Latios (Mega Evolution in ORAS).

## Villainous teams

| Team | Leader | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team Magma | Maxie | Expand the land (awaken Groudon) |
| Team Aqua | Archie | Expand the sea (awaken Kyogre) |

- Ruby: the player faces Magma; Sapphire: Aqua; Emerald and ORAS: both.
- ORAS (Delta Episode): Zinnia tries to use Rayquaza to divert a meteor.

## Real-world parallels

- Hoenn = Kyūshū; Mt. Chimney recalls the Sakurajima volcano.
- The Mossdeep Space Center recalls the Tanegashima space center.
