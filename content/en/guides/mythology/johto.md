## The region

- Based on Kansai and part of Chūbu, Japan (Ecruteak City ≈ Kyōto, Goldenrod City ≈ Ōsaka).
- Games: Gold/Silver/Crystal and HeartGold/SoulSilver.
- Lore built around Ecruteak's two towers, the Unown and tradition (Kimono Girls, Kurt's Apricorns, the dragon clan).

## The legend of the towers

- Ecruteak had two towers: the Brass Tower (Lugia's home) and the Tin Tower (Ho-Oh's home).
- About 150 years ago lightning set the Brass Tower on fire (today the Burned Tower).
- Three nameless Pokémon died in the fire; Ho-Oh revived them as Raikou, Entei and Suicune.
- Lugia left the tower for the depths of the Whirl Islands; Ho-Oh also left.
- HGSS: the Kimono Girls dance to call Lugia/Ho-Oh with the Tidal Bell/Clear Bell.

## Other legends

- **Ruins of Alph:** panels with Kabuto, Omanyte, Aerodactyl and Ho-Oh; once solved, they reveal the Unown.
- **Celebi:** guardian of Ilex Forest and time traveler; the forest shrine is dedicated to it.
- **Dragon's Den:** Blackthorn's dragon clan (Clair, Lance) and the Elder's test.
- **Lake of Rage:** Team Rocket forces evolutions with radio waves; hence the Red Gyarados (first fixed shiny).
- **Sinjoh Ruins (HGSS, event):** links Johto to Sinnoh; with Arceus, gives Dialga, Palkia or Giratina.

## Trios

- **Legendary beasts:** Raikou, Entei and Suicune. Roaming: they flee on turn 1, HP does not recover.
- **Tower duo:** Lugia and Ho-Oh, Johto's "pair".
- HGSS: the Hoenn trio (Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza) appears at the Embedded Tower with postgame orbs.

## Real-world parallels

- The Tin Tower and Brass Tower recall Kyōto's pagodas.
- Goldenrod is a commercial metropolis like Ōsaka; the Magnet Train links it to Saffron (Tokyo), like the bullet train.
- The Kimono Girls and the Dance Theater recall Kyōto's geisha and theater.
