## The region

- Archipelago based on Hawaii: Melemele, Akala, Ula'ula, Poni and the artificial island Aether Paradise.
- Games: Sun/Moon and Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon. No Gyms: the Island Challenge (trials and kahunas).
- Core lore: the Tapus, the sun and the moon, and the Ultra Beasts.

## Deities and the Island Challenge

- Each island has a Tapu, a semi-divine guardian that protects and also punishes.
- Each island has a kahuna (chosen by the Tapu) and trial captains.
- Trials give Z-Crystals; the Tapu gives the Sparkling Stone used in the Z-Ring.

## Sun and Moon

- Alola's people revere the "beast that calls the sun" (Solgaleo) and the "beast that calls the moon" (Lunala).
- Cosmog ("Nebby") comes from another world through Ultra Wormholes, evolves into Cosmoem and then into the version legend.
- The Altar of the Sunne/Moone, on Poni, is the ritual site with the flutes (Sun Flute and Moon Flute).
- **Necrozma** stole the light of Ultra Megalopolis (USUM); it fuses with Solgaleo or Lunala (Dusk Mane / Dawn Wings) and
  becomes Ultra Necrozma.

## Ultra Beasts

- Beings from other worlds that arrive through Ultra Wormholes; dangerous to Alola.
- Nihilego, Buzzwole, Pheromosa, Xurkitree, Celesteela, Kartana, Guzzlord (SM, with Looker in the postgame).
- USUM: Poipole/Naganadel, Stakataka and Blacephalon.
- Caught with Beast Balls.

## Real-world parallels

- Alola = Hawaii; Melemele ≈ Oahu, Akala ≈ Maui, Ula'ula ≈ Hawaii (Big Island), Poni ≈ Kauai.
- "Tapu" comes from a Polynesian term for the sacred and forbidden.
- Alolan forms reflect adaptation to the islands' climate.
