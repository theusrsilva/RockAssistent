## How to complete

- Trade with the other version via Wireless Adapter or cable.
- Most Johto Pokémon only appear on the Sevii Islands, postgame.
- Hoenn Pokémon: trade with Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald (after the Network Machine).
- Raikou, Entei or Suicune: one per game, based on your starter.
