## What's new

- Gen 3 mechanics: Abilities, Natures, IVs/EVs, split Special Attack and Special Defense.
- Sevii Islands: extra story and a new postgame.
- Wireless Adapter in the box: trades and battles without a cable.
- Teachy TV, Fame Checker, VS Seeker and Running Shoes.
- National Pokédex after the Hall of Fame (60 species seen).
- Trading with Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald after giving Celio the Ruby and Sapphire.
