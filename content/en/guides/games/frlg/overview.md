## Quick facts

- Shipped with the Wireless Adapter: trades and battles without a cable.
- The National Pokédex comes after the Hall of Fame, with 60 Pokémon seen.
- Trading with Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald needs the Network Machine on One Island fixed (postgame).
