## Tips

- **Blue** (Rival, Oak's Lab, Pallet Town): The rival takes the starter with the type edge; lower his stats, then attack.
- **Blue** (Rival, Route 22): Pidgey 9 and his starter 9: optional, but good money.
- **Brock** (Gym Leader, Pewter Gym): Onix 14 with Bide and Rock Tomb: one-shot it with Water/Grass.
- **Blue** (Rival, Cerulean City (north exit, before Nugget Bridge)): At the north exit. Pidgeotto 17, Abra, Rattata and starter 18.
- **Misty** (Gym Leader, Cerulean Gym): Starmie 21 with Water Pulse: Electric or Grass; bring Paralyze Heals.
- **Blue** (Rival, S.S. Anne 2F): On 2F. Raticate, Kadabra and starter Lv. 20.
- **Lt. Surge** (Gym Leader, Vermilion Gym): Find the 2 switches in the trash cans. Raichu 24: Ground (Diglett).
- **Erika** (Gym Leader, Celadon Gym): Vileplume and Victreebel 29: Fire/Flying/Psychic and Awakenings.
- **Giovanni** (Team Rocket Boss, Rocket Hideout B4F): Onix 25, Rhyhorn 24 and Kangaskhan 29: Water/Grass/Fighting.
- **Blue** (Rival, Pokémon Tower 2F): On 2F. Five Pokémon up to Lv. 25.
- **Blue** (Rival, Silph Co. 7F): Starter Lv. 40: heal first.
- **Giovanni** (Team Rocket Boss, Silph Co. 11F): Nidoqueen 41: Water/Ground/Psychic.
- **Koga** (Gym Leader, Fuchsia Gym): Weezing 43 uses Self-Destruct: Psychic/Ground.
- **Sabrina** (Gym Leader, Saffron Gym): Alakazam 43: Dark/Bug or strong physical moves.
- **Blaine** (Gym Leader, Cinnabar Gym): Arcanine 47: Water/Ground/Rock.
- **Giovanni** (Gym Leader, Viridian Gym): Ground team: Water, Grass and Ice.
- **Blue** (Rival, Route 22): On Route 22. Starter Lv. 53.
- **Lorelei** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Ice/Water: Electric and Fighting.
- **Bruno** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Fighting/Rock: Psychic, Water and Flying.
- **Agatha** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Ghost/Poison: Psychic and Ground.
- **Lance** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Dragons and Gyarados: Ice and Electric.
- **Blue** (Champion, Indigo Plateau): Mixed team: Electric, Ice and Water.
- **Admin** (Team Rocket Admin, Rocket Warehouse, Five Island): Muk 52, Arbok 53, Vileplume 54. Psychic and Ground.
- **Admin** (Team Rocket Admin, Rocket Warehouse, Five Island): Golbat, Weezing and Houndoom 55: Water/Rock/Electric.
- **Lorelei** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Rematch after helping Lorelei and restoring the Network Machine; Lv. 63–66.
- **Bruno** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Rematch with Steelix; Lv. 65–68.
- **Agatha** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Rematch with Crobat and Misdreavus; Lv. 65–70.
- **Lance** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Rematch with Kingdra; Lv. 66–72. Ice handles almost everything.
- **Blue** (Champion, Indigo Plateau): Rematch with Heracross and Tyranitar; Lv. 72–75.
