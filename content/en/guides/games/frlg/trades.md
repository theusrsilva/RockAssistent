## Gifts

- Starter Lv. 5: Oak's Lab.
- Magikarp Lv. 5: Mt. Moon Pokémon Center (₽500).
- Eevee Lv. 25: Celadon Mansion.
- Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan Lv. 25: Fighting Dojo.
- Lapras Lv. 25: Silph Co. 7F.
- Togepi (egg): Water Labyrinth, Five Island.

## Fossils

- Dome Fossil (Kabuto) or Helix Fossil (Omanyte): Mt. Moon.
- Old Amber (Aerodactyl): Pewter Museum (Cut).
- Revive at Cinnabar Lab (Lv. 5).

## Game Corner (Celadon)

- FireRed: Abra, Clefairy, Dratini, Scyther, Porygon.
- LeafGreen: Abra, Clefairy, Pinsir, Dratini, Porygon.
