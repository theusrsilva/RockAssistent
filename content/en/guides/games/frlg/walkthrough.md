Story order, from Pallet Town to the Sevii Islands. Each stop: what to do, items, encounters and boss.

## Pallet Town

**Do**

- Take the Potion from your bedroom PC.
- Pick Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle (Lv. 5).
- Bring Oak's Parcel to Oak: Pokédex and Poké Balls.
- Get the Town Map at the rival's house.

**Items**

- Potion
- Pokédex
- Town Map

**Encounters**

:::encounters frlg pallet-town

**Boss**

:::boss frlg rival-1

- Tip: The rival takes the starter with the type edge; lower his stats, then attack.

## Route 1

**Do**

- Get a free Potion from the Poké Mart clerk.
- Catch Pidgey or Rattata once you have Poké Balls.

**Encounters**

:::encounters frlg route-1

**Do**
