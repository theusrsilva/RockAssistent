- **Mystic Ticket** (event): leads to Navel Rock; Lugia and Ho-Oh Lv. 70.
- **Aurora Ticket** (event): leads to Birth Island; Deoxys Lv. 30 (Attack Forme in FR, Defense Forme in LG).
- The tickets came through Mystery Gift at events and stores.
- Altering Cave: Mystery Gift was meant to change its species, but the event never came out; only Zubat.
