## After the Hall of Fame

1. See the 150 Sinnoh Dex entries and talk to Oak in Sandgem: National Pokédex and Poké Radar.
2. Gym leader rematches (once a day), Bebe's Eevee, daily outbreaks, expanded Trophy Garden.
3. Ramanas Park, Turnback Cave (Giratina), Fullmoon Island (Cresselia), Snowpoint Temple (Regigigas).
4. New Victory Road section (with Marley), Morimoto at Hotel Grand Lake, Elite Four rematch 1.
5. Battle Zone by ship from Snowpoint: Fight Area → Survival Area → Stark Mountain (Heatran) → Resort Area → Battle Park.
6. After the National Dex: Barry (Battle Tower, Saturday and Sunday) and Dawn/Lucas (once a week). After Heatran: Elite Four rematch 2.

## National Pokédex

- Requirement: see the 150 Sinnoh Dex entries (Manaphy doesn't count) and beat the Elite Four.
- Unlocks the Super Rod (Fight Area), Rotom, and new Pokémon in the Grand Underground and the Great Marsh.

## Battle Tower

- Single (3 Pokémon) and Double (4); lv 50; 7 battles per round; no duplicate items.
- Tower Tycoon Palmer at the 21st and 49th battle (Single).
- Beating Palmer at the 49th unlocks Master Class: Ranks 1–10 with leaders, Elite Four and Cynthia.
- Banned: Mewtwo, Mew, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Celebi, Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza, Jirachi, Deoxys, Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Phione, Manaphy, Darkrai, Shaymin, Arceus.

## Grand Underground

- Hideaways change with the National Dex: Kanto–Hoenn starters, Dratini, Larvitar [BD], Bagon [SP], Beldum and many from outside the Sinnoh Dex.
- Levels scale with your badges.

## Ramanas Park

- Trade Mysterious Shards (3 small or 1 large per Slate) for Slates.
- Discovery Slate → Regirock, Regice, Registeel; the other Slates unlock in stages (see Legendaries).
- Giratina Origin lv 100: battle only; afterwards you get the Griseous Orb.
