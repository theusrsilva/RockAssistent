## Version differences

- 37 exclusive Pokémon in each version (list in Exclusives).
- Dialga, the Johto beasts and Ho-Oh in Brilliant Diamond; Palkia, the Kanto birds and Lugia in Shining Pearl.
- Sinnoh fossil: Skull Fossil (Cranidos) in BD, Armor Fossil (Shieldon) in SP.

## What's new

- Grand Underground with Hideaways: visible Pokémon, some from outside the Sinnoh Dex.
- HMs became TMs (TM93–TM100) and the Pokétch uses the field move without teaching it (Flash is still a TM).
- Ramanas Park replaces Pal Park: older legendaries via Slates.
- Exp. Share always on; Fairy type; Pokémon follow you.
- Super Contest Show and Poffins; Ball Capsules with stickers.
- Hyper Training (Bottle Caps) and the Judge in the Battle Tower.
