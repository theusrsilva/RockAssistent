## Tips

- **Barry (rival):** always has the starter strong against yours; cover that weakness before each fight.
- **Roark (Rock):** Water, Grass, Fighting or Steel; Cranidos (lv 14) hits hard.
- **Gardenia (Grass):** Fire, Flying, Ice; Roserade (lv 22) is the ace.
- **Maylene (Fighting):** Flying and Psychic; Lucario (Fighting/Steel) falls to Fire and Ground.
- **Crasher Wake (Water):** Grass against Quagsire, Electric against Gyarados.
- **Fantina (Ghost):** Dark and Ghost; Normal and Fighting don't connect.
- **Byron (Steel):** Fire, Fighting, Ground; Bronzor and Bronzong float (Levitate).
- **Candice (Ice):** Fire, Fighting, Rock, Steel; Abomasnow brings snow.
- **Volkner (Electric):** Ground; watch out for Octillery (Water).
- **Mars / Jupiter / Saturn:** Purugly falls to Fighting; Skuntank only to Ground; Toxicroak to Psychic.
- **Cyrus:** Fighting against Sneasel/Weavile; Electric and Rock against the flyers (Honchkrow, Crobat, Gyarados).
- **Aaron (Bug):** Fire, Flying, Rock; Drapion only falls to Ground.
- **Bertha (Ground):** Water, Grass, Ice; Hippowdon is the ace.
- **Flint (Fire):** Water, Ground, Rock; mixed team (Steelix, Drifblim, Lopunny).
- **Lucian (Psychic):** Dark, Ghost, Bug; Bronzong only falls to Fire and Ground.
- **Cynthia:** Garchomp (lv 66) takes 4× from Ice and also falls to Fairy; Spiritomb has no weakness (Fairy works).
- **Rematches:** leaders once a day with the National Dex; Elite Four and Cynthia in 3 tiers (up to lv 88).
