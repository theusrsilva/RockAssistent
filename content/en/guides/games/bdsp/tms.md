## TMs

## Tutors

- Draco Meteor: east of Celestic Town (Dragon with high friendship).
- Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn, Hydro Cannon: Route 228 (Battle Zone desert).
- Move Reminder: Pastoria City (Heart Scale). Move Deleter: Canalave City.
