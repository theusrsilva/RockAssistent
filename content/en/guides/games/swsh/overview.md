## Version differences

- Cover legendary: Zacian (Sword) or Zamazenta (Shield).
- Gyms 4 and 6: Bea and Gordie (Sword); Allister and Melony (Shield).
- Different exclusive Pokémon and Max Lair legendaries (see Exclusives).

## What's new

- Dynamax and Gigantamax (3 turns, only in stadiums and Max Raids).
- Wild Area: open area with weather and Max Raid Battles for up to 4 players.
- Pokémon visible in the overworld; Galarian forms.
- TRs (single-use moves) alongside TMs; Exp. Share always on.
- Pokémon Camp and curry; Rotom Bike to ride on water.
