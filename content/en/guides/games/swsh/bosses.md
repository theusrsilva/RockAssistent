## Tips

- **Hop (rival):** takes the starter weak to yours; at the end he has the other version's Zacian/Zamazenta.
- **Bede:** Psychic team, later Fairy; Steel and Poison work all game.
- **Marnie:** Dark with Morpeko; Fighting and Fairy; G-Max Grimmsnarl loses to Steel and Poison.
- **Milo (Grass):** Fire, Flying, Bug, Ice.
- **Nessa (Water):** Grass and Electric; Drednaw takes 4× from Grass.
- **Kabu (Fire):** Water, Rock, Ground.
- **Bea (Fighting, Sword):** Psychic, Flying, Fairy.
- **Allister (Ghost, Shield):** Dark and Ghost; watch out for Mimikyu's Disguise.
- **Opal (Fairy):** Steel and Poison.
- **Gordie (Rock, Sword):** Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground, Steel.
- **Melony (Ice, Shield):** Fire, Fighting, Rock, Steel.
- **Piers (Dark):** Fighting, Fairy, Bug; no Dynamax.
- **Raihan (Dragon):** double battle with sandstorm; Ice, Fairy, Fighting against Duraludon.
- **Oleana and Rose (Macro Cosmos):** G-Max Garbodor loses to Ground; G-Max Copperajah (Steel) loses to Fire, Fighting, Ground.
- **Eternatus:** Poison/Dragon; Ground and Psychic. The capture is mandatory and guaranteed.
- **Leon (Champion):** G-Max Charizard last; bring Water/Rock and Ice/Fairy for the dragons. His starter is the one strong against yours.
- **Sordward and Shielbert:** Steel and Fighting; Fire and Ground.
- **Klara and Avery (Isle of Armor):** Galarian Slowbro; Ground and Dark.
- **Mustard:** G-Max Urshifu of the opposite style; Fairy and Flying against both.
- **Honey and Peony:** Lv 60–75 teams; Peony uses Steel.
