## After the Hall of Fame

- **Master Ball:** from Magnolia.
- **Hop and Sordward/Shielbert:** Slumbering Weald, then rampaging Dynamax Pokémon in the stadiums (Turffield → Hulbury → Motostoke → Wedgehurst → Stow-on-Side → Ballonlea → Circhester → Hammerlocke).
- **Zacian/Zamazenta:** at the top of the Energy Plant; beat the other version's one and catch yours (Lv 70).
- **Final Hop battle:** Slumbering Weald; unlocks the Battle Tower.
- **Eternatus:** already caught in the story (Lv 60); stays on your team or in the box.
- **Battle Tower:** Lv 50 ranked battles, BP and prizes (Mints, Bottle Caps, Ability Capsule).
- **Charmander with Gigantamax:** Leon's room.
- **Champion Cup again:** invite up to 10 trainers with Rare League Cards.
- **Wild Area and Max Raids:** catch with no level cap; Lake of Outrage; Watt Traders.
- **Shiny Charm:** Hotel Ionia, with a complete Galar Pokédex.
- **Dailies:** Marnie (Spikemuth), Morimoto (Hotel Ionia).
