- **Mystery Gift**: unlock it by answering "LINK TOGETHER WITH ALL" in the Petalburg questionnaire.
- **Eon Ticket**: Southern Island (Latios/Latias Lv. 50); outside Japan only by Record Mixing with Ruby/Sapphire.
- **Mystic Ticket**: Navel Rock (Lugia and Ho-Oh Lv. 70).
- **Aurora Ticket**: Birth Island (Deoxys Lv. 30).
- **Old Sea Map**: Faraway Island (Mew Lv. 30); distributed in Japan only (Pokémon Festa, 18 Jul–21 Aug 2005).
