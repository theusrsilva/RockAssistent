- 7 facilities: Tower, Dome, Palace, Arena, Factory, Pike and Pyramid.
- Frontier Pass at the entrance; Battle Points buy items and TMs.
- Sudowoodo Lv. 40 (Wailmer Pail) and a Skitty → Meowth trade.
- Each Frontier Brain awards a Silver, then a Gold Symbol.

**Items**

- Frontier Pass

**Bosses**

:::boss e anabel-silver

- Tip: Battle Tower. Alakazam, Entei and Snorlax: Dark and Fighting.

:::boss e anabel-gold

- Tip: Raikou, Latios and Snorlax: Ground for Raikou, Ice/Dragon for Latios.

:::boss e tucker-silver

- Tip: Battle Dome. Swampert, Salamence and Charizard: Grass and Ice.

:::boss e tucker-gold

- Tip: Swampert, Metagross and Latias.

:::boss e spenser-silver

- Tip: Battle Palace (Nature decides). Crobat, Slaking and Lapras.

- Tip: Arcanine, Slaking and Suicune.

:::boss e greta-silver

- Tip: Battle Arena (3 turns). Heracross, Umbreon and Shedinja: bring Fire/Rock/Ghost for Shedinja.

:::boss e greta-gold

- Tip: Umbreon, Gengar and Breloom.

:::boss e lucy-silver

- Tip: Battle Pike. Seviper, Shuckle and Milotic.

- Tip: Seviper, Steelix and Gyarados.

:::boss e brandon-silver

- Tip: Battle Pyramid. The 3 Regis: Fighting, Fire and Water.

:::boss e brandon-gold

- Tip: Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres: Rock and Electric.
