## Missing from Emerald (trade only)

- Surskit, Masquerain
- Meditite, Medicham
- Roselia
- Zangoose (from Ruby)
- Lunatone (from Sapphire)

## Differences from Ruby/Sapphire

- Seedot and Lotad, Sableye and Mawile in the same game.
- Groudon and Kyogre (Lv. 70) and Rayquaza on one cartridge.
- Roaming Latios or Latias: Mom asks your favorite color (red = Latias, blue = Latios).
- Johto: gift starters and new Safari Zone species (postgame).

## How to complete

- Trade with Ruby/Sapphire (cable) or FireRed/LeafGreen (cable or Wireless Adapter).
