## Gifts

- Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip Lv. 5: Prof. Birch's bag, Route 101.
- Wynaut Egg: hot springs old lady, Lavaridge.
- Castform Lv. 25 (holding Mystic Water): Weather Institute.
- Beldum Lv. 5: Steven's house, Mossdeep (postgame).
- Chikorita, Cyndaquil or Totodile Lv. 5: Prof. Birch, after completing the Hoenn Dex.

## Fossils

- Root Fossil (Lileep) or Claw Fossil (Anorith): top of Mirage Tower; the other in Desert Underpass (postgame).
- Revive at Devon Corporation (Rustboro): Lv. 20.

## Static encounters

- Kecleon Lv. 30: Routes 119 and 120 (Devon Scope).
- Voltorb Lv. 25: New Mauville; Electrode Lv. 30: Aqua Hideout.
- Sudowoodo Lv. 40: Battle Frontier.

## Eggs
