## Version differences

- Black 2: Black City, Black Tower, Black Kyurem story, Challenge Mode key, Zekrom and Latios.
- White 2: White Forest, White Treehollow, White Kyurem story, Easy Mode key, Reshiram and Latias.
- Full list in Exclusives.

## What's new

- New Unova Dex with 300 Pokémon, old and new mixed from the start.
- Pokémon World Tournament (PWT) with leaders and champions from every region.
- Hidden Grottoes (Hidden Abilities), Medals, Join Avenue and Pokéstar Studios.
- Key System (Challenge/Easy Mode, extra areas) and Memory Link with a Black/White save.
- Kyurem fusion with the DNA Splicers (Black Kyurem and White Kyurem).
