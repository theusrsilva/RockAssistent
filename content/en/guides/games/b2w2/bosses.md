## Tips

- **Hugh (Aspertia)**: Lv 5; basic attacks.
- **Hugh (Floccesy Ranch)**: train to Lv 9-10 first.
- **Cheren (Aspertia)**: Fighting moves help; hit Lillipup before Work Up.
- **Roxie (Virbank)**: Psychic or Ground; Whirlipede has Poison Point.
- **Burgh (Castelia)**: Fire or Flying.
- **Colress (Route 4)**: Fire or Ground for Magnemite and Klink.
- **Ingo and Emmet (Nimbasa)**: multi battle with a partner; Water for Boldore, Flying for Gurdurr.
- **Elesa (Nimbasa)**: Ground except on Emolga.
- **Rood (Driftveil)**: two Pokémon Lv 27; he gives Zorua afterwards.
- **Clay (Driftveil)**: Water or Grass.
- **Skyla (Mistralton)**: Electric, Rock or Ice.
- **Hugh (Undella)**: three Pokémon Lv 39-41.
- **Zinzolin (Lacunosa)**: double with Hugh; Fire or Fighting.
- **Drayden (Opelucid)**: Ice or Dragon.
- **Zinzolin and Shadow Triad (Opelucid)**: Fire for Cryogonal, Fighting for Pawniard.
- **Marlon (Humilau)**: Grass or Electric.
- **Plasma Frigate and Giant Chasm**: Zinzolin, Colress and three Shadow Triad fights back to back; heal between them.
- **Ghetsis**: Hydreigon Lv 52 last.
- **Hugh (Victory Road)**: four Pokémon Lv 55-57.
- **Shauntal**: Dark moves.
- **Grimsley**: Fighting moves.
- **Caitlin**: Bug, Dark or Ghost.
- **Marshal**: Flying or Psychic.
- **Iris (Champion)**: Ice for the Dragons; Fighting for Aggron and Lapras.
- **N (N's Castle)**: one Lv 70 legendary dragon.
- **Shadow Triad (Icirrus)**: single, Triple and Rotation battles, once per season.
- **Cilan, Chili and Cress**: daily double battle in Striaton.
- **Alder (Floccesy)**: Rock for Volcarona.
- **Cheren and Bianca (Memory Link)**: need a cleared Black/White save linked.
- **Hugh (postgame)**: Undella, then Driftveil.
- **Colress (P2 Laboratory)**: Fire or Ground; Wash Rotom is Water/Electric.
- **N (seasonal)**: N's Castle, team changes with the season.
- **Cynthia**: Undella, spring and summer.
- **Morimoto and Nishino**: Game Freak building, Castelia.
- **Elite Four and Iris rematches**: Lv 72-78.
