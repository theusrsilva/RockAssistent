## Unova Link and Key System

- Unova Link (C-Gear menu) moves Keys, Memory Link and Dream Radar data.
- Keys unlocked after the League: Challenge Mode (Black 2) or Easy Mode (White 2).
- Tower/Treehollow Key opens the other version's Black Tower or White Treehollow.
- City Key changes Black City into White Forest (and the reverse).
- Iron Key (Registeel) and Iceberg Key (Regice) open the missing Underground Ruins room.
- Each game unlocks only its own Keys; the other version's Keys come from a friend's game via Unova Link.

## Memory Link

- Link a Black/White save on another DS (C-Gear → Unova Link).
- Unlocks flashback scenes with Cheren, Bianca, N and others.
- Postgame battles with Cheren (Route 5) and Bianca (Route 1) with their B/W teams.

## Facilities

- Pokémon World Tournament (Driftveil): Driftveil, Unova, Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, World, Champions and Mix tournaments.
- Battle Subway, Battle Institute (Nimbasa), Black Tower / White Treehollow.
