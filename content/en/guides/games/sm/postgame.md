## After the Hall of Fame

- Party in Iki Town; Tapu Koko at the Ruins of Conflict; credits.
- Hau gives the Z-Crystals of the other two starters.
- Tapu Lele, Tapu Bulu and Tapu Fini in the ruins of the other islands.
- Cosmog at the altar's lake (alternate world).
- Gladion gives Type: Null at Aether Paradise.
- Dexio gives the Key Stone (Mega Evolution).
- Elite Four rematch and title defenses.

## Ultra Beasts (Looker and Anabel)

Starts at the Akala Motel (Route 8). Wicke and Looker give 10 Beast Balls per mission.

| Mission | Ultra Beast | Level | Location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UB-01 | Nihilego | 55 | Wela Volcano Park or Diglett's Tunnel |
| UB-02 | Buzzwole (Sun) / Pheromosa (Moon) | 65 / 60 | Melemele Meadow / Verdant Cavern |
| UB-03 | Xurkitree | 65 | Lush Jungle or Memorial Hill |
| UB-04 | Kartana (Sun) / Celesteela (Moon) | 60 / 65 | Route 17 and Malie Garden / Haina Desert and Malie Garden |
| UB-05 | Guzzlord | 70 | Resolution Cave |

- Looker's final reward: 1,000,000 Pokédollars.
- After the 5 missions: Necrozma (lv 75) at Ten Carat Hill.

## Battle Tree

- Past the Poni Gauntlet. On the first visit, battle Red or Blue.
- Lv 50, no repeated items or species. Single (3), Double (4), Multi (2).
- Battle Legends (Red and Blue) after a 20-win streak; Super mode afterwards.
- BP buys competitive items and Mega Stones.

| Win streak | Prize |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | Rare Candy |
| 50 | Ability Capsule |
| 100 | Lansat Berry |
| 200 | Starf Berry |

## Title defense

- Each return to the League: Elite Four (lv 63) and a random challenger.
- Challengers: Hau, Gladion, Plumeria, Sophocles, Molayne, Hapu, Ryuki, Tristan, Kukui; Faba only on the 1st of each month.

## Island Scan

- The QR Scanner gives 20 points per code; at 100 points, Island Scan reveals a rare Pokémon for 1 minute on the island you are on.
- The Pokémon changes by island and weekday (e.g. Cyndaquil on Melemele on Sunday, Totodile on Melemele on Monday).
