## Version differences

- Sun follows the 3DS clock; Moon runs 12 hours ahead (day on the console is night in the game).
- Cover legendary: Solgaleo (Sun) and Lunala (Moon). Different Ultra Beasts in the postgame.
- First trial Totem: Gumshoos (Sun) or Alolan Raticate (Moon). Details in Exclusives.

## What's new

- No gyms: trials and Z-Moves (Z-Ring + Z-Crystal, 1 per battle).
- Alolan forms, Ultra Beasts, SOS battles and Battle Royal.
- Poké Ride replaces HMs; Poké Pelago and Pokémon Refresh.
- Mega Evolution only in the postgame (Dexio's Key Stone).
