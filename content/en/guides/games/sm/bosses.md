## Tips

- **Hau (rival):** takes the starter weak to yours; later Alolan Raichu, an Eeveelution and Komala.
- **Ilima / Totem Gumshoos or Raticate:** Normal; Fighting moves.
- **Hala:** Fighting; Flying and Psychic.
- **Gladion:** Type: Null/Silvally is Normal until it becomes the type strong against your starter; Fighting early on.
- **Totem Wishiwashi (Lana):** Grass and Electric; break School Form with big damage.
- **Totem Salazzle (Kiawe):** Ground is 4x; Psychic and Water too.
- **Totem Lurantis (Mallow):** Fire, Flying, Ice; watch the Trumbeak ally.
- **Olivia:** Rock; Water, Grass, Fighting, Steel.
- **Totem Vikavolt (Sophocles):** Fire and Rock.
- **Totem Mimikyu (Acerola):** break Disguise with a weak move; Ghost and Steel.
- **Nanu:** Dark; Fighting, Bug, Fairy.
- **Plumeria and Guzma (Team Skull):** Poison (Psychic, Ground) and Bug (Fire, Flying, Rock).
- **Faba and Lusamine (Aether):** Lusamine has a mixed team; Bewear (Normal/Fighting) hits hard.
- **Hapu:** Ground; Water, Grass, Ice.
- **Totem Kommo-o:** Fairy is 4x; Flying, Psychic, Ice.
- **Elite Four:** Hala (Fighting), Olivia (Rock), Acerola (Ghost), Kahili (Flying).
- **Kukui (Champion):** mixed team with the starter strong against yours; fast Lycanroc.
- **Title defense:** random challenger (Faba is drawn like the others).
