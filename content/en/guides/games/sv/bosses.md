## Tips

- **Nemona (rival):** always takes the starter that beats yours; her Pawmi/Pawmot (Electric) stays on the team all game.
- **Katy (Bug):** Fire, Flying or Rock; Teddiursa becomes Tera Bug.
- **Brassius (Grass):** Fire, Flying, Bug or Ice; Sudowoodo becomes Tera Grass and loses its Water weakness.
- **Iono (Electric):** Ground handles the team, but Mismagius (Tera Electric) has Levitate: use Dark or Ghost on it.
- **Kofu (Water):** Grass or Electric; Crabominable (Tera Water) hits hard with Fighting moves.
- **Larry (Normal):** Fighting; Staraptor becomes Tera Normal.
- **Ryme (Ghost, double battle):** Dark or Ghost; Toxtricity becomes Tera Ghost.
- **Tulip (Psychic):** Bug, Ghost or Dark; Florges becomes Tera Psychic.
- **Grusha (Ice):** Fire, Fighting, Rock or Steel; Altaria becomes Tera Ice and loses its Dragon and Fairy weaknesses.
- **Titans:** Klawf (Rock) falls to Water/Grass; Bombirdier (Flying/Dark) to Rock/Electric/Ice/Fairy; Orthworm (Steel) to Fire/Fighting/Ground; Great Tusk to Water/Psychic/Flying/Fairy; Iron Treads to Water/Fire/Fighting/Ground; Dondozo to Grass/Electric and Tatsugiri to Dragon/Fairy.
- **Team Star:** Giacomo (Dark), Mela (Fire), Atticus (Poison), Ortega (Fairy), Eri (Fighting). The Starmobile is a Revavroom (Steel/Poison): Ground is 4×.
- **Arven (Path of Legends finale):** Mabosstiff Tera Dark last; bring Fighting, Bug or Fairy.
- **Clavell:** mixed team that changes with your starter; his ace is the starter that beats yours.
- **Penny:** six Eeveelutions; Sylveon becomes Tera Fairy (Poison or Steel).
- **Elite Four:** Rika (Ground: Water/Grass/Ice), Poppy (Steel: Fire/Fighting/Ground), Larry (Flying: Electric/Ice/Rock), Hassel (Dragon: Ice/Dragon/Fairy).
- **Geeta (Top Champion):** mixed team; Glimmora becomes Tera Rock and Kingambit takes 4× from Fighting.
- **AI Sada/AI Turo:** six Paradox Pokémon at Lv. 66–67; the final Koraidon/Miraidon is fought with your own legendary.
- **Kieran (Kitakami):** starts with Yanma and Poliwhirl; later Dipplin and Gliscor; bring Ice and Flying.
- **Ogerpon:** four forms in a row (Fire, Water, Rock and Grass); a Flying or Poison Pokémon helps against several.
- **Carmine:** Sinistcha (Grass/Ghost) is her ace; Fire, Flying or Dark.
- **BB League Elite Four:** Crispin (Fire), Amarys (Steel), Lacey (Fairy), Drayton (Dragon); all double battles.
- **Kieran (BB League Champion, double):** Hydrapple last; Incineroar and Grimmsnarl bring Intimidate and screens.
- **Terapagos:** Titan-style fight with Kieran; its type changes as it loses HP.
- **Mochi Mayhem:** Yukito/Hideko, Arven/Penny and Nemona at Lv. 80–87; then Pecharunt (Poison/Ghost: Ground, Psychic, Ghost, Dark).
- **Ogre Clan:** eight themed bosses (weather, Electric, Fairy, Rock/Ice, Normal, Fighting, Ghost/Dark); Lv. 75–80.
