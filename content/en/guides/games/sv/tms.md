## TMs

The game has 229 TMs: 001–171 in the base game, 172–200 in The Teal Mask and 201–229 in The Indigo Disk. Every TM can be used any number of times.

:::tms sv

## Where to find them

**TM Machine**
- Found in every Pokémon Center (and in the Terarium plazas).
- Each recipe costs LP plus materials dropped by Pokémon you defeat or catch.
- A recipe unlocks when you obtain that TM for the first time (ground, reward or gift); badges and Team Star bases also unlock recipes.
- From The Teal Mask onward, you can filter TMs by the Pokémon that learns them.
