## In-game trades

The base game has no NPC trades. In The Indigo Disk post-game, each Special Coach in the League Club offers one trade on the 3rd conversation.

- You give a Pokémon you caught yourself (not shiny, not legendary/mythical).
- The one you get carries the Partner Ribbon and the coach's OT.
