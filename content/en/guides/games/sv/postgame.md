## The Way Home and the Hall of Fame

- Unlocks after the 3 storylines (Victory Road, Path of Legends and Starfall Street): Zero Gate → Area Zero → Zero Lab.
- Credits → Master Ball (Clavell), IV judge in the Box and Koraidon/Miraidon form switching.
- Area Zero stays open: wild Paradox Pokémon and a 2nd Koraidon/Miraidon (Lv. 72) that goes to the Box.

- **Tournament:** the first run is Arven, Jacq, Dendra and Geeta; later opponents vary (Nemona, Penny, teachers, Clavell).

## The Teal Mask (DLC, Kitakami)

- Access via the school trip; opens right after the Treasure Hunt starts.
- Story: Carmine and Kieran, the festival, the Loyal Three and Ogerpon's test.
- Post-story: wild Loyal Three, Ogre Clan (8 bosses, rewards like an Ability Patch), Billy & O'Nare, Perrin and Bloodmoon Ursaluna.
- Kitakami Dex 200 → Glimmering Charm.

## The Indigo Disk (DLC, Blueberry Academy)

- Requires finishing the 3 storylines, The Way Home and The Teal Mask.
- BB League: doubles Elite Four (Crispin, Amarys, Lacey, Drayton) and Champion Kieran.
- Area Zero Underdepths → Terapagos Lv. 85 (guaranteed catch).
- Post-story: free flight (Amarys's supplement), Special Coaches (Lv. 80+ rematches and trades), Cyrano (secret boss), Item Printer, Synchro Machine.
- Blueberry Dex 200 → new Paradox Pokémon (Perrin); 240 → Mark Charm.

## Mochi Mayhem epilogue

- Requires the Mythical Pecha Berry (Mystery Gift), the 1st Academy Ace Tournament and both DLCs.
- Double battles alongside Kieran (Yukito/Hideko, Arven/Penny) and the possessed Nemona.
- Pecharunt Lv. 88 (guaranteed catch) at Loyalty Plaza.
- Afterwards: Carmine, Kieran, Nemona, Arven and Penny become Special Coaches.
