## Version differences

- **Scarlet:** Naranja Academy, Professor Sada, Koraidon, ancient Paradox Pokémon (Great Tusk, Flutter Mane...).
- **Violet:** Uva Academy, Professor Turo, Miraidon, future Paradox Pokémon (Iron Treads, Iron Valiant...).
- About 17 exclusive Pokémon per version in the base game, plus more in the DLCs (see Exclusives).

## What's new

- Open world with no numbered routes; fixed levels per area and a ride Pokémon (Koraidon/Miraidon).
- Terastal: changes a Pokémon's type with the Tera Orb; Tera Raid Battles for up to 4 players.
- Let's Go/Auto Battle, Picnics with sandwiches (Meal Powers) and the TM Machine (TMs crafted with materials + LP).
- Union Circle: explore with up to 3 friends; online or local trades and battles.
