## Tips

- **Barry (Route 203)**: His starter has the type edge over yours: lead with Starly/Bidoof chip damage or overlevel your starter to 10+.
- **Roark (Oreburgh Gym)**: Turtwig/Piplup sweep. Chimchar: learn Mach Punch at 14 or use Shinx/Machop/Budew.
- **Mars (Valley Windworks)**: Purugly hits hard: use a Fighting type (Machop, Monferno) or Shinx for Zubat.
- **Gardenia (Eterna Gym)**: Roserade is the threat: Fire (Monferno, Ponyta) or Flying (Staravia) wins.
- **Jupiter (Team Galactic Eterna Building)**: Skuntank only fears Ground: Geodude/Onix line or Psychic-free bulk.
- **Barry (Hearthome City (east exit, Route 209))**: Bring a counter to his starter and a Fighting/Electric type for Staravia.
- **Maylene (Veilstone Gym)**: Flying (Staravia) and Psychic beat her; Lucario resists a lot, use Fire or Ground on it.
- **Crasher Wake (Pastoria Gym)**: Grass or Electric. Gyarados (Intimidate) dodges Ground; Quagsire only fears Grass.
- **Barry (Pastoria City)**: Same team as before, a few levels higher: lead with your counter to his starter.
- **Fantina (Hearthome Gym)**: Dark types (Murkrow, Stunky) hit all three; Normal moves fail. Gengar knows Hypnosis.
- **Barry (Canalave City)**: Heracross joined: Flying or Fire handles it; keep a counter for his starter.
- **Byron (Canalave Gym)**: Fire, Water, Fighting or Ground. Bronzor (Levitate) dodges Ground.
- **Candice (Snowpoint Gym)**: Fire, Fighting, Rock or Steel. Medicham is the odd one: Flying or Psychic for it.
- **Saturn (Lake Valor (Valor Cavern))**: Toxicroak hates Psychic and Ground; Kadabra is frail to Dark/Ghost.
- **Mars (Lake Verity)**: Fighting for Purugly, Fire for Bronzor, Electric for Golbat.
- **Cyrus (Team Galactic HQ, Veilstone City)**: Sneasel and Murkrow fall to Fighting; Golbat to Electric/Ice/Rock.
- **Saturn (Team Galactic HQ, Veilstone City)**: Same trio as Lake Valor, a bit stronger.
- **Mars (Spear Pillar, Mt. Coronet)**: Double battle: spread moves (Surf, Earthquake on a Flying ally) help.
- **Jupiter (Spear Pillar, Mt. Coronet)**: Ground for Skuntank; KO the Golbats fast.
- **Cyrus (Spear Pillar, Mt. Coronet)**: Weavile (4× Fighting weak) and Gyarados (4× Electric weak) are his best: answer both.
- **Volkner (Sunyshore Gym)**: Ground types, but Octillery and Ambipom are not Electric: bring a Fighting type too.
- **Barry (Pokémon League (before the Elite Four))**: Six Pokémon now (Snorlax added): Fighting for Snorlax.
- **Aaron (Pokémon League)**: Fire and Flying sweep the Bugs; Drapion needs Ground.
- **Bertha (Pokémon League)**: Water and Grass. Hippowdon sets sand.
- **Flint (Pokémon League)**: Water and Ground; Drifblim and Lopunny are not Fire, plan for them.
- **Lucian (Pokémon League)**: Dark, Ghost and Bug. Bronzong is the hardest (Fire works).
- **Cynthia (Pokémon League)**: Ice for Garchomp (4×). Spiritomb has no weakness. Bring Full Restores.
- **Barry (Fight Area)**: Full six, Lv 58–64: same counters as the League fight.
