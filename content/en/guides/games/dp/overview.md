## Version differences

- Diamond: Dialga on the box; Pearl: Palkia.
- Each version has its own wild Pokémon (see Exclusives).

## What's new

- 107 new Pokémon (National Dex up to 493).
- Physical/special split: each move has its own category.
- Wi-Fi play: GTS, trades and battles online (servers now closed).
- Underground (fossils, Spheres, secret bases), Pokétch and Poké Radar.
- Day/night and weekday events by the DS clock.
- Pal Park: bring Pokémon from the GBA games (6 per day).
