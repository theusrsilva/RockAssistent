Story path through Sinnoh in order. Levels and teams in the boss cards.

## Twinleaf Town → Route 202

**Do**

- Leave home with Barry and head to Lake Verity.
- Pick Turtwig, Chimchar or Piplup from the briefcase to escape the wild Starly.
- Visit Prof. Rowan in Sandgem Town; get the Pokédex.
- Your assistant (Lucas/Dawn) gives 5 Poké Balls and teaches catching on Route 202.

**Items**

- Running Shoes (Mom, after Sandgem)
- Journal (Mom)
- Poké Ball ×5 (assistant)

**Encounters**

:::encounters dp twinleaf-town

:::encounters dp route-201

:::encounters dp lake-verity

:::encounters dp route-202

## Jubilife City → Route 203

**Do**

- Deliver the Parcel to Barry at the Trainers' School.
- Find the 3 clowns and answer their quizzes to get the Pokétch.
- Route 203 leads east to Oreburgh Gate.

**Items**

- Town Map (Barry)
- Pokétch (Pokétch Company)
- TM10 Hidden Power (Jubilife)
- Old Rod (Fisherman in the west gate of Jubilife, towards Route 218)
- Quick Claw (Jubilife Condominiums, 1F)

**Encounters**

:::encounters dp route-203

**Boss**

:::boss dp rival-1

- Tip: His starter has the type edge over yours: lead with Starly/Bidoof chip damage or overlevel your starter to 10+.

## Oreburgh Gate → Oreburgh Mine

**Do**

- Get HM06 Rock Smash from the Hiker at Oreburgh Gate.
- Find Roark in Oreburgh Mine, then challenge his Gym.
- Catch Geodude/Onix in the mine if you need a Rock or Ground type.

**Items**

- HM06 Rock Smash (Hiker, Oreburgh Gate)
- TM01 Focus Punch (Oreburgh Gate B1F, needs Rock Smash)
- TM76 Stealth Rock (Roark)
- Coal Badge

**Encounters**

:::encounters dp oreburgh-gate

:::encounters dp oreburgh-city

:::encounters dp oreburgh-mine

**Boss**

:::boss dp roark

- Tip: Turtwig/Piplup sweep. Chimchar: learn Mach Punch at 14 or use Shinx/Machop/Budew.

## Route 207 → Floaroma Meadow

**Do**

- Back in Jubilife, help your assistant beat two Galactic Grunts (tag battle).
- Cross Route 204 (Rock Smash in Ravaged Path) to Floaroma Town.
- Beat the Grunts in Floaroma Meadow to get the Works Key.
- Open Valley Windworks and beat Commander Mars.

**Items**

- TM09 Bullet Seed (Route 204)
- TM39 Rock Tomb and TM03 Water Pulse (Ravaged Path)
- TM88 Pluck (Floaroma Town)
- Works Key and Honey (Floaroma Meadow)
- Sprayduck (Floaroma flower shop)

## Team Galactic Eterna Building → Old Chateau

**Do**

- Raid the Galactic building to rescue the Clefairy and beat Jupiter.
- Get the Bicycle from the Eterna cycle shop afterwards.
- Optional: Old Chateau (Cut, Route 205) has Gastly and Rotom (postgame, at night).

**Items**

- TM46 Thief (Galactic building)
- Bicycle (Eterna)
- TM90 Substitute (Old Chateau)

**Encounters**

**Boss**

## Route 206 → Amity Square

- Hearthome: Fan Club, Contest Hall, Amity Square.

**Items**

- TM26 Earthquake (Wayward Cave hidden part)
- Odd Keystone (Route 208, Black Belt hidden in the trees)
- Poffin Case (Hearthome Fan Club)
- TM43 Secret Power and TM45 Attract (Amity Square)

- Challenge Crasher Wake; Barry waits by the Great Marsh.

**Items**

- TM87 Swagger (Pokémon Mansion)
- HM05 Defog (Great Marsh, girl in the marsh)

## Lake Valor

**Do**

- Lake Valor was bombed: beat Saturn in Valor Cavern.
- Rush to Lake Verity and beat Mars to save Mesprit.
- Meanwhile Jupiter takes Uxie from Lake Acuity.
- Then go to Veilstone: the Galactic HQ is open.

**Items**

- TM25 Thunder (Lake Valor, Diamond/Pearl)
- TM38 Fire Blast (Lake Verity)

**Encounters**

:::encounters dp lake-valor

**Boss**

:::boss dp saturn-lake-valor

:::boss dp mars-lake-verity

## Galactic Warehouse → Team Galactic HQ

**Do**

- A Grunt drops the Storage Key in front of the Galactic building (after Lake Acuity); use it on the Galactic Warehouse in Veilstone.
- Get HM02 Fly in the warehouse; take the Galactic Key.
- Beat Cyrus (he gives you the Master Ball) and then Saturn to free the lake trio.

**Items**

- HM02 Fly (Galactic Warehouse)
- TM21 Frustration, TM36 Sludge Bomb, TM49 Snatch (warehouse/HQ)
- Master Ball (Cyrus)

**Boss**

:::boss dp cyrus-galactic-hq

- Tip: Sneasel and Murkrow fall to Fighting; Golbat to Electric/Ice/Rock.

:::boss dp saturn-galactic-hq

- Tip: Same trio as Lake Valor, a bit stronger.

- Double battle: Mars + Jupiter, Barry on your side.
- Beat Cyrus, then catch Dialga (Diamond) or Palkia (Pearl), Lv 47.

**Items**

- Adamant Orb (Diamond) / Lustrous Orb (Pearl), Spear Pillar
- TM02 Dragon Claw, TM69 Rock Polish, TM80 Rock Slide (Mt. Coronet)

**Encounters**

:::encounters dp spear-pillar

**Boss**

:::boss dp mars-spear-pillar

- Tip: Double battle: spread moves (Surf, Earthquake on a Flying ally) help.

:::boss dp jupiter-spear-pillar

:::boss dp cyrus-spear-pillar

## Route 223 → Pokémon League

**Do**

- Surf and Waterfall up Route 223.
- Victory Road needs Surf, Strength, Rock Smash, Waterfall and Rock Climb (no fog).
- Barry waits before the Elite Four. Heal and stock up: no breaks between battles.

**Items**

- TM41 Torment, TM59 Dragon Pulse, TM71 Stone Edge, TM79 Dark Pulse (Victory Road)

**Encounters**

:::encounters dp route-223

:::encounters dp victory-road

:::encounters dp pokemon-league

**Boss**

:::boss dp rival-5

- Tip: Six Pokémon now (Snorlax added): Fighting for Snorlax.

:::boss dp aaron

- Tip: Fire and Flying sweep the Bugs; Drapion needs Ground.

:::boss dp bertha

- Tip: Water and Grass. Hippowdon sets sand.

:::boss dp flint

- Tip: Water and Ground; Drifblim and Lopunny are not Fire, plan for them.

:::boss dp lucian

- Tip: Dark, Ghost and Bug. Bronzong is the hardest (Fire works).

:::boss dp cynthia

- Tip: Ice for Garchomp (4×). Spiritomb has no weakness. Bring Full Restores.
