## Gifts

- Turtwig, Chimchar or Piplup (Lake Verity).
- Happiny Egg (Hiker, Route 209 near Hearthome).
- Riolu Egg (Riley, Iron Island).
- Eevee (Bebe, Hearthome City; after the National Dex).

## Fossils

- Dig them in the Underground (Explorer Kit, Eterna City).
- Before the National Dex: Skull Fossil (Diamond) or Armor Fossil (Pearl).
- After the National Dex: Helix, Dome, Old Amber, Root and Claw Fossils too.
- Revive at the Oreburgh Mining Museum.

## Eggs

- Happiny and Riolu Eggs (above). Day Care: Solaceon Town.
