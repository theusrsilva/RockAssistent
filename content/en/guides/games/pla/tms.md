## No TMs

The game has no TMs or HMs. Moves come from three sources:

- **Level-up:** each species learns its own list as it levels; you can swap moves freely from the menu (nothing is forgotten for good).
- **Move Tutor (Zisu):** at the Training Grounds, Jubilife Village. Teaches moves for money; the list grows with your Galaxy Team rank (new moves at 3★ and 6★).
- **Move Mastery:** mastered moves get the Agile Style (faster, weaker) and Strong Style (stronger, acts later). Each style costs extra PP.

## Mastery

- Level-up moves: mastered on their own at a set level, or earlier with a Seed of Mastery.
- Tutor moves: only get the styles after you give Zisu a Seed of Mastery.
- Seeds of Mastery: Alphas, Requests, Path of Solitude, Spiritomb wisp rewards.
