## Single version

There are no version-exclusive Pokémon. The only choice is the clan in M17:

| Choice | First legendary | Later (Origin Forme) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diamond Clan (Adaman) | Dialga | Palkia Origin |
| Pearl Clan (Irida) | Palkia | Dialga Origin |

## Hisuian forms

Growlithe, Arcanine, Voltorb, Electrode, Typhlosion, Qwilfish, Sneasel, Samurott, Lilligant, Zorua, Zoroark, Braviary, Sliggoo, Goodra, Avalugg, Decidueye. Also Basculin (White-Striped) and Dialga/Palkia Origin.

## Other differences

- Evolution from the menu; trade evolutions replaced by the Linking Cord.
- No eggs, no abilities in battle and no held items.
- To bring Pokémon to other games, use Pokémon HOME.
