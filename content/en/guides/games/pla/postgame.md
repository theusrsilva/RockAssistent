## Postgame order

1. Credits after M18. In M19, Volo becomes a legends guide and Laventon gives you the other two starters.
2. M20: Alpha Vespiquen (Grueling Grove, Stone Plate). Cogita, at the Ancient Retreat, opens the next missions.
3. M21: Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf in the lake caverns (first, the Alphas Goodra, Overqwil and Zoroark).
4. M22 Heatran, M23 Cresselia, M24 Regigigas, M25 Kamado at Prelude Beach (Fist Plate).
5. M26: Pixie Plate from Cogita. Volo meets you at the Sacred Plaza and battles you at the Temple of Sinnoh; Giratina comes right after (Spooky Plate, Azure Flute).
6. Req91 Giratina Origin, Req94 Forces of Nature, complete the Pokédex.
7. M27: Arceus with the Azure Flute.

## Collections

- **Unown:** 28 forms spread across the regions; once caught, a form also appears in the Solaceon Ruins.
- **Spiritomb:** 107 wisps (Req22, Vessa); at the end, Spiritomb 60 in the Shrouded Ruins at night.
- **Plates:** 18 (plus Arceus's Legend Plate).

## Alphas and distortions

- After calming each region's Noble, its regular Pokémon can also appear as Alphas.
- Space-time distortions: Pokémon from other regions, Shards and rare items (Upgrade, Dubious Disc). After the story, the starters' Hisuian final forms appear (Decidueye, Typhlosion, Samurott).

## Massive Mass Outbreaks (Daybreak)

- Unlocked through Req95–102. Give Aguav Berries to Mai's Munchlax to reveal where they are.
- Several spots in one region at once; higher odds of shiny and Alpha.

## Shiny Charm and challenges

- **Shiny Charm:** with every Pokédex entry at research level 10, talk to Laventon (Galaxy Hall).
- **Noble rematches:** Lv. 70, after the main story (talk to the warden at the arena); rewards: 3–4 Exp. Candy M, plus an Exp. Candy L or Grit Pebbles if you take no damage.
- **Ingo (Training Grounds):** rematches, Path of Tenacity (three trainers in a row) and Path of Solitude (one Pokémon only, Req108–122).
- **Eternal Battle Reverie:** back-to-back battles with Arceus's challenges, Lv. 60 to 100.
- **Rank 10★:** 50,000 research points (20 Rare Candy and 10 Grit Rock).
