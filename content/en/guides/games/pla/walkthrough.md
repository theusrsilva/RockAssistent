## How to read

- No gyms: each expedition region has a frenzied Noble Pokémon (its "boss"). Calming it gives a Plate and opens the next step.
- Region access by Galaxy Team rank: Crimson Mirelands 2★, Cobalt Coastlands 3★, Coronet Highlands 4★, Alabaster Icelands 5★.
- Rides: Wyrdeer (runs), Basculegion (swims), Ursaluna (digs), Sneasler (climbs), Hisuian Braviary (glides).
- "M" = Mission (story); "Req" = Request (optional side quest).

## Jubilife Village

**Do**
- Pick your starter at Galaxy Hall: Rowlet, Cyndaquil or Oshawott.
- Join the Galaxy Team (Survey Corps); every expedition starts here.
- Services: Training Grounds (Zisu, Ingo), Trading Post (Simona), Craftworks (Anvin), General Store, Pastures, Farm.
- Unown W, ? and H and 7 Spiritomb wisps are in and around the village.

:::boss pla akari-1
