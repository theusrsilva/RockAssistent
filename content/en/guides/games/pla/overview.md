## Version differences

- Single version: no version-exclusive Pokémon. Choosing the Diamond Clan or the Pearl Clan only decides whether Dialga or Palkia comes first (both can be caught).

## What's new

- Catching without battling: throw the Poké Ball straight at Pokémon on the map.
- Alphas (bigger, red eyes) and Noble Pokémon calmed with balms.
- Pokédex built from research tasks (research level 10 completes an entry).
- Agile and Strong Styles on mastered moves; no TMs and no abilities in battle.
- Hisuian forms and new species: Wyrdeer, Kleavor, Ursaluna, Basculegion, Sneasler, Overqwil, Enamorus.
- Free 1.1.0 Daybreak update (February 2022): Massive Mass Outbreaks and more postgame challenges.
