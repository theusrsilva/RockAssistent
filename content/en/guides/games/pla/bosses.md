## Tips

- **Noble Pokémon:** dodge attacks (roll with Y), throw balms until it is stunned, then battle to drain its frenzy gauge.
- **Noble Kleavor (Bug/Rock):** Water, Rock and Steel.
- **Noble Lilligant (Grass/Fighting):** Flying (x4), Fire, Ice, Poison, Psychic, Fairy.
- **Noble Arcanine (Fire/Rock):** Water and Ground (x4).
- **Noble Electrode (Electric/Grass):** Fire, Ice, Poison, Bug.
- **Noble Avalugg (Ice/Rock):** Fighting and Steel (x4).
- **Akari/Rei (rival):** Pikachu always last; bring Ground.
- **Volo:** Togepi and Gible early on; at the reveal, Garchomp (Ice x4) and Spiritomb (Fairy). Giratina follows with no healing.
- **Wardens (Mai, Lian, Melli, Ingo, Gaeric, Sabi):** teams of 1 to 3 Pokémon; Ingo tests Sneasler.
- **Clan leaders (Adaman, Irida):** Leafeon (Fire, Flying) and Glaceon (Fire, Fighting, Rock, Steel), always with another Eeveelution in double or triple battles.
- **Miss Fortune Sisters (Coin, Clover, Charm):** Toxicroak (Psychic x4), Abomasnow (Fire x4), Rhydon (Water/Grass x4).
- **Beni and Kamado:** Lv. 59–62 teams; Psychic for Sneasler, Fighting for Golem and Snorlax.
- **Dialga/Palkia Origin:** same balm fight; then Dragon does the job.
- **Arceus:** Lv. 75; bring Fighting and plenty of balms.
