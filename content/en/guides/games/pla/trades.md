## In-game trades

- There are no NPC trades. The Trading Post (Simona, Training Grounds entrance, Jubilife Village) handles trades with other players and sells evolution items for Merit Points.
- Some Requests ask for a Pokémon and keep it: Wurmple (Req1), a large Buizel (Req4), Geodude (Req12), Petilil (Req29), Parasect (Req33), Croagunk (Req34), Nosepass (Req68), a Fire-type Pokémon (Req81).
