## Version differences

- Single version: no exclusives (see Exclusives).
- Story centered on Giratina and the Distortion World.
- Gym order changed: Fantina is the 3rd Gym.

## What's new

- Regional Dex of 210, with Pokémon from both Diamond and Pearl.
- Giratina Origin Forme (Griseous Orb), Shaymin Sky Forme (Gracidea), Rotom forms.
- Battle Frontier: Tower, Factory, Arcade, Castle and Hall.
- Wi-Fi Plaza and Vs. Recorder; Battleground for Gym Leader rematches.
- New areas (Distortion World, Battleground) and updated trainer teams.
