## Tips

- **Barry (Route 201)**: One Lv 5 starter with the type edge: just attack, he has no healing.
- **Barry (Route 203)**: Starly 7 plus his starter 9: a Lv 10+ starter or Shinx/Bidoof support is enough.
- **Roark (Oreburgh Gym)**: Turtwig/Piplup win easily; Chimchar uses Mach Punch (learned at Lv 14).
- **Mars (Valley Windworks)**: Fighting (Machop, Monferno) for Purugly; Electric or Rock for Zubat.
- **Gardenia (Eterna Gym)**: Roserade (Lv 22) is the threat: Fire or Flying (Staravia).
- **Jupiter (Team Galactic Eterna Building)**: Skuntank is weak only to Ground: Geodude/Onix help.
- **Fantina (Hearthome Gym)**: Dark types (Murkrow, Stunky) beat her; Normal and Fighting moves fail.
- **Barry (Hearthome City (east exit, Route 209))**: Keep a counter for his starter; Staravia falls to Electric/Rock.
- **Maylene (Veilstone Gym)**: Flying and Psychic; Lucario takes Fire/Ground/Fighting super effectively.
- **Barry (Pastoria City)**: His starter is Lv 36: bring the counter.
- **Crasher Wake (Pastoria Gym)**: Grass and Electric. Quagsire only fears Grass.
- **Cyrus (Celestic Town (ruins))**: Fighting for Sneasel and Murkrow; Electric or Rock for Golbat.
- **Barry (Canalave City)**: Five Pokémon, starter at 38: Fire/Flying for Heracross.
- **Byron (Canalave Gym)**: Fire, Fighting, Ground or Water. Magneton is 4× weak to Ground.
- **Saturn (Lake Valor (Valor Cavern))**: Ground or Psychic for Toxicroak; Electric for Golbat.
- **Mars (Lake Verity)**: Fighting for Purugly, Fire for Bronzor.
- **Candice (Snowpoint Gym)**: Fire, Fighting, Rock, Steel. Froslass (Ice/Ghost) dodges Fighting.
- **Cyrus (Team Galactic HQ, Veilstone City)**: Fighting for Sneasel; Crobat falls to Electric/Ice/Psychic; Honchkrow to Electric/Ice/Rock.
- **Saturn (Team Galactic HQ, Veilstone City)**: Same trio as Lake Valor, a little stronger.
- **Mars (Spear Pillar, Mt. Coronet)**: Spread moves help; KO Purugly with Fighting.
- **Jupiter (Spear Pillar, Mt. Coronet)**: Ground for Skuntank (Bronzor is immune: watch Levitate).
- **Cyrus (Distortion World)**: Weavile (4× Fighting) and Gyarados (4× Electric): bring answers; Houndoom falls to Water/Ground.
- **Volkner (Sunyshore Gym)**: Ground types win; Electivire has Thunder Punch and Fire Punch.
- **Barry (Pokémon League (before the Elite Four))**: Six Pokémon with Snorlax: Fighting for it, counter for his starter.
- **Aaron (Pokémon League)**: Fire and Rock sweep; Drapion needs Ground.
- **Bertha (Pokémon League)**: Water and Grass; Gliscor needs Ice (4×).
- **Flint (Pokémon League)**: Water and Ground; Infernape also fears Psychic/Flying.
- **Lucian (Pokémon League)**: Dark, Ghost and Bug; Bronzong takes Fire.
- **Cynthia (Pokémon League)**: Ice (4×) for Garchomp; Spiritomb has no weakness; Togekiss is new here.
- **Flint (Fight Area)**: Water/Ground spread (Surf, Earthquake with a Flying ally) covers both.
- **Volkner (Fight Area)**: Ground for all three; watch Electivire's Fire Punch and Brick Break.
- **Mars (Stark Mountain)**: Double with Buck: Fighting for Purugly, Fire for Bronzong.
- **Jupiter (Stark Mountain)**: Ground for Skuntank, Electric for Golbat.
- **Barry (Survival Area (outside the Battleground))**: Lv 59–65: same counters, more levels.
- **Barry (Survival Area (outside the Battleground))**: Lv 79–85 after 10 wins at the League: bring a Lv 80+ team.
- **Aaron (Pokémon League)**: Fire and Rock again; Scizor is 4× weak to Fire.
- **Bertha (Pokémon League)**: Water and Grass; Ice for Gliscor.
- **Flint (Pokémon League)**: Water and Ground.
- **Lucian (Pokémon League)**: Dark and Ghost; Gallade fears Flying/Ghost.
- **Cynthia (Pokémon League)**: Ice for Garchomp, a Lv 75+ team.
- **Roark (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Water/Grass; Tyranitar fears Fighting (4×).
- **Gardenia (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Fire, Flying, Ice.
- **Fantina (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Dark and Ghost; Dusknoir is bulky.
- **Maylene (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Flying and Psychic; Breloom fears Flying (4×).
- **Crasher Wake (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Grass and Electric; Ludicolo needs Flying/Poison.
- **Byron (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Fire and Ground; Skarmory needs Electric/Fire.
- **Candice (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Fire, Fighting, Steel.
- **Volkner (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Ground; Lanturn needs Grass.
- **Cheryl (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Wobbuffet counters: avoid it or use Dark moves.
- **Buck (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Shuckle is a wall: Water/Steel for it.
- **Marley (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Fast team: Rock and Electric cover most.
- **Mira (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Dark and Ghost moves; Magnezone fears Ground.
- **Riley (Battleground, Survival Area)**: Ice for Salamence (4×); Fire/Ground for Metagross.
