## Gifts

- Turtwig, Chimchar or Piplup (Route 201).
- Togepi Egg (Cynthia, Eterna cycle shop).
- Eevee Lv 20 (Bebe, Hearthome City).
- Porygon Lv 25 (house north of the Veilstone Pokémon Center).
- Riolu Egg (Riley, Iron Island).

## Fossils

- Dig them in the Underground (Explorer Kit, Eterna City).
- Before the National Dex: Skull or Armor Fossil (by Trainer ID).
- After the National Dex: Helix, Dome, Old Amber, Root and Claw too.
- Revive at the Oreburgh Mining Museum.

## Eggs

- Togepi and Riolu Eggs (above). Day Care: Solaceon Town.
