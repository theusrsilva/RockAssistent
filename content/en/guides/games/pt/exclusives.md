Platinum is a single version: it has no exclusive Pokémon against a pair.

## Compared with Diamond / Pearl

- Both Dialga and Palkia can be caught (postgame, Spear Pillar).
- Diamond-only and Pearl-only species (Murkrow, Misdreavus, Larvitar, Bagon...) are mixed into Platinum's Dex.
- Rotom's five forms change in its house in the Galactic building (Eterna), no event needed.
- Regirock, Regice and Registeel can be caught in-game (see Legendaries).
