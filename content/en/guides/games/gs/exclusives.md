## Gold only

- Mankey, Primeape, Growlithe, Arcanine, Spinarak, Ariados, Gligar, Teddiursa, Ursaring, Mantine

## Silver only

- Vulpix, Ninetales, Meowth, Persian, Ledyba, Ledian, Delibird, Skarmory, Phanpy, Donphan

## Ho-Oh and Lugia

- Gold: Ho-Oh Lv. 40 in the story; Lugia Lv. 70 in the postgame.
- Silver: Lugia Lv. 40 in the story; Ho-Oh Lv. 70 in the postgame.

## Goldenrod Game Corner

- Gold: Ekans. Silver: Sandshrew.

## How to complete

- Trade with the other version or Crystal via Link Cable.
- Gen 1 Pokémon missing here (Kanto starters, legendary birds, Mewtwo, fossils): Time Capsule with Red/Blue/Yellow.
