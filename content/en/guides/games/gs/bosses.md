## Tips

- **Silver** (Rival, Cherrygrove City): Starter Lv. 5 with type advantage over yours. Losing is fine: the story goes on.
- **Li** (Elder, Sprout Tower 3F, Violet City): Bellsprout 7 ×2 and Hoothoot 10: Fire or Flying.
- **Falkner** (Gym Leader, Violet Gym): Pidgeotto 9: Onix (trade), Geodude or Electric (Mareep).
- **Bugsy** (Gym Leader, Azalea Gym): Scyther 16 with Fury Cutter: Fire, Flying or Rock.
- **Silver** (Rival, Azalea Town (west exit)): Gastly 12, Zubat 14 and starter 16: bring a Normal type and a counter to his starter.
- **Whitney** (Gym Leader, Goldenrod Gym): Miltank 20 (Rollout, Milk Drink): traded Machop or Geodude; paralyze it.
- **Silver** (Rival, Burned Tower, Ecruteak City): Haunter 20 and Magnemite 18: Fire/Ground and Dark; starter 22.
- **Morty** (Gym Leader, Ecruteak Gym): Gengar 25 with Hypnosis: Bite/Crunch and Psychic; bring Awakenings.
- **Chuck** (Gym Leader, Cianwood Gym): Poliwrath 30 (Hypnosis): Flying, Psychic or Electric.
- **Jasmine** (Gym Leader, Olivine Gym): Steelix 35: Fire, Water, Ground or Fighting; Magnemite falls to Ground/Fire.
- **Kiyo** (Karate King, Mt. Mortar): Optional. Hitmonlee and Hitmonchan 34: Flying or Psychic.
- **Executive** (Team Rocket Executive, Team Rocket Hideout B3F, Mahogany Town): Zubat, Raticate and Koffing 22–24: Electric/Psychic.
- **Executive** (Team Rocket Executive, Team Rocket Hideout B2F, Mahogany Town): Arbok, Gloom and Murkrow 23–25, alongside Lance.
- **Pryce** (Gym Leader, Mahogany Gym): Piloswine 31: Fire, Water, Grass or Fighting.
- **Executive** (Team Rocket Executive, Radio Tower 4F, Goldenrod City): A single Golbat Lv. 36: Electric, Ice or Rock.
- **Executive** (Team Rocket Executive, Radio Tower 5F, Goldenrod City (fake director)): Fake director: five Koffing and Weezing; Psychic or Ground (watch for Selfdestruct).
- **Silver** (Rival, Goldenrod Underground (Basement Key door)): Starter Lv. 32 and Sneasel: Fire/Fighting for Sneasel.
- **Executive** (Team Rocket Executive, Radio Tower 5F, Goldenrod City): Arbok, Vileplume and Murkrow 32: Psychic, Fire and Electric.
- **Executive** (Team Rocket Executive, Radio Tower 5F, Goldenrod City (director's office)): Houndoom 35: Water, Ground or Fighting.
- **Clair** (Gym Leader, Blackthorn Gym): Kingdra 40 is only weak to Dragon: Ice for the Dragonair, heavy hitters for Kingdra.
- **Silver** (Rival, Victory Road): Starter Lv. 38, Sneasel and Haunter: Fighting and Psychic help.
- **Will** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Psychic: Dark, Bug and Ghost.
- **Koga** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Poison: Psychic and Ground; Fire for Forretress.
- **Bruno** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Fighting: Psychic and Flying; Water for Onix.
- **Karen** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Dark: Fighting and Bug; Fire for Vileplume.
- **Lance** (Champion, Indigo Plateau): Three Dragonite: Ice; Electric for Gyarados, Rock/Water for Charizard.
- **Lt. Surge** (Gym Leader, Vermilion Gym): Electabuzz 46 and Raichu 44: Ground (works on Electrode and Magneton too).
- **Sabrina** (Gym Leader, Saffron Gym): Espeon, Mr. Mime and Alakazam 48: Dark or Bug.
- **Erika** (Gym Leader, Celadon Gym): Tangela, Jumpluff, Victreebel and Bellossom: Fire, Ice or Flying.
- **Janine** (Gym Leader, Fuchsia Gym): Crobat, Weezing ×2, Ariados and Venomoth 39: Psychic or Ground.
- **Misty** (Gym Leader, Cerulean Gym): Starmie 47 and Lapras: Electric; Quagsire is only weak to Grass.
- **Brock** (Gym Leader, Pewter Gym): Onix, Graveler, Omastar, Kabutops 44: Grass and Fighting (Water won't dent Omastar/Kabutops).
- **Silver** (Rival, Mt. Moon): Gengar, Alakazam and starter 45: Dark and strong hits.
- **Blaine** (Gym Leader, Seafoam Islands (temporary gym)): Magcargo, Magmar and Rapidash 50: Water (Magcargo takes 4×).
- **Blue** (Gym Leader, Viridian Gym): Gyarados, Rhydon, Arcanine and Exeggutor 58: Electric, Water, Ice and Fire coverage.
- **Silver** (Rival, Indigo Plateau (Pokémon Center)): Optional, postgame. Crobat 48 and starter 50.
- **Red** (Pokémon Trainer, Mt. Silver (summit)): Pikachu 81: Ground. Bring a Lv. 70+ team and plenty of Full Restores.
