## Gifts

- Starter Lv. 5: Prof. Elm, New Bark Town.
- Mystery Egg (Togepi): Mr. Pokémon, Route 30; Elm's aide returns it in Violet.
- Spearow with mail: Goldenrod north gate (bring it to Route 31).
- Eevee Lv. 20: Bill, Goldenrod City.
- Shuckle Lv. 15: house in Cianwood City.
- Tyrogue Lv. 10: Karate King, Mt. Mortar.
- Elm gives an Everstone when you show him Togepi.

## Game Corner

- Goldenrod: Abra, Ekans (Gold) / Sandshrew (Silver), Dratini.
- Celadon: Mr. Mime, Eevee, Porygon.

## Eggs

- Route 34 Day-Care: male + female of the same group (or Ditto).
- Eggs hatch at Lv. 5.
- No fossils to revive in this generation.
