- **Mystery Gift** (Game Boy Color infrared): unlocked by the woman on Goldenrod Dept. Store 5F; up to 5 exchanges per day, one per partner, giving items and decorations.
- **Pokémon Stadium 2** also sends one gift per day through Mystery Gift (Transfer Pak).
- **Celebi**: official events only: Japan, the Celebi Tour in Europe (Aug–Nov 2001), the Pokémon Fun Fest in the US/Canada (2001) and Australia; the Ilex Forest shrine is where it appears in Crystal.
- **Mew**: only by trading an event Mew from Gen 1 (Time Capsule).
