Story order: Johto (8 badges and the League), then Kanto (8 more). Each stop: what to do, items, encounters and boss.

## New Bark Town

**Do**

- Set the clock: time and weekday change encounters and events.
- Get the PokéGear from your mom.
- Pick Chikorita, Cyndaquil or Totodile from Prof. Elm.
- Mom can save part of your money.

**Items**

- PokéGear
- Potion

**Encounters**
