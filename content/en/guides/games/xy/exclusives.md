## Other differences

- Mega Stones: Charizardite X and Mewtwonite X (X); Charizardite Y and Mewtwonite Y (Y). They can be traded.
- Sundial Mega Stones: Pinsirite, Manectite, Tyranitarite (X); Heracronite, Houndoominite, Aggronite (Y).
- Friend Safari ignores exclusivity: any species your friends' safaris offer can be caught.
