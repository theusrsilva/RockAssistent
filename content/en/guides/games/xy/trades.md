## Gifts

- Chespin, Fennekin or Froakie: Aquacorde Town.
- Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle (Lv. 10) with its Mega Stone: Prof. Sycamore, Lumiose City.
- Lucario (Lv. 32) with Lucarionite: Korrina, Tower of Mastery.
- Lapras (Lv. 30): first person on Route 12.
- Snorlax (Lv. 15): static on Route 7 (Poké Flute).

## Fossils

- Glittering Cave (story): Jaw Fossil (Tyrunt) or Sail Fossil (Amaura); pick one.
- Glittering Cave, Rock Smash: Old Amber; after the League also Skull, Armor, Cover, Plume, plus Root/Claw (X) or Helix/Dome (Y).
- Revive them at the Fossil Lab, Ambrette Town (Lv. 20).
