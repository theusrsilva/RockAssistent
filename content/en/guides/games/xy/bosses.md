All story and postgame bosses, in order. The walkthrough shows each one with its moves.

:::bosses xy

## Tips

- **Shauna** (Rival, Aquacorde Town): Any starter move works; she has a single level 5 Pokémon.
- **Viola** (Gym Leader, Santalune Gym): Fire or Flying for Vivillon; Fletchling from Route 2 helps a lot.
- **Professor Sycamore** (Pokémon Professor, Pokémon Lab, Lumiose City): Three level 10 Kanto starters: use your starter's type advantage.
- **Tierno** (Rival, Route 5): Grass or Electric beat his Corphish.
- **Tierno & Trevor** (Rival, Route 7): Double battle on Route 7: hit Corphish with Grass/Electric first.
- **Grant** (Gym Leader, Cyllage Gym): Climb the wall; Water, Grass, Fighting or Steel handle Tyrunt and Amaura.
- **Korrina** (Gym Leader, Geosenge Town): Two Lucario with a partner: Fire, Fighting and Ground moves hit hard.
- **Serena** (Rival, Tower of Mastery, Shalour City): Rival: Absol is weak to Fighting and Fairy.
- **Korrina** (Gym Leader, Shalour Gym): Flying and Psychic beat her team; Hawlucha is fast, hit it first.
- **Korrina** (Gym Leader, Tower of Mastery, Shalour City): You use the Lucario she just gave you; Mega Evolve it at once (the battle restarts if you don't).
- **Serena** (Rival, Coumarine City): Same core team as before, three levels higher.
- **Ramos** (Gym Leader, Coumarine Gym): Fire, Flying, Ice or Poison; Gogoat can heal with Leech Seed.
- **Team Flare Admin** (Team Flare, Kalos Power Plant): Houndoom: Water, Ground, Rock or Fighting.
- **Aliana** (Team Flare, Kalos Power Plant): Single Mightyena: Fighting or Fairy.
- **Clemont** (Gym Leader, Lumiose Gym (Prism Tower)): Ground moves; Heliolisk (Dry Skin) is weak to Fighting too.
- **Serena** (Rival, Route 14): Their starter is fully evolved now; bring its weakness.
- **Valerie** (Gym Leader, Laverre Gym): Steel and Poison; Mawile resists both, so use Fire or Ground on it.
- **Team Flare Admin** (Team Flare, Poké Ball Factory): Fighting beats Scraggy; Water or Ground for Houndoom.
- **Celosia** (Team Flare, Poké Ball Factory): Double with Bryony: Ground on Manectric.
- **Bryony** (Team Flare, Poké Ball Factory): Fighting or Fairy on Liepard.
- **Mable** (Team Flare, Frost Cavern): Houndoom: Water or Ground.
- **Serena** (Rival, Anistar City): Four Pokémon now, including an Eeveelution.
- **Olympia** (Gym Leader, Anistar Gym): Dark, Ghost or Bug; Meowstic is the strongest.
- **Lysandre** (Team Flare, Lysandre Labs): Fairy and Fighting help; Electric for Gyarados.
- **Aliana** (Team Flare, Lysandre Labs): Fairy or Ice on Druddigon.
- **Celosia** (Team Flare, Lysandre Labs): Ground beats both Manectric and Drapion.
- **Bryony** (Team Flare, Lysandre Labs): Fighting beats Liepard and Bisharp.
- **Mable** (Team Flare, Lysandre Labs): Fighting on Weavile, Water on Houndoom.
- **Xerosic** (Team Flare, Lysandre Labs): Bug or Fairy on Malamar; Electric or Psychic on Crobat.
- **Lysandre** (Team Flare, Team Flare Secret HQ): Same team, slightly stronger.
- **Lysandre** (Team Flare, Team Flare Secret HQ): Mega Gyarados is Water/Dark: Electric or Grass; use the box legendary.
- **Professor Sycamore** (Pokémon Professor, Couriway Town): Venusaur, Charizard and Blastoise at level 50: cover Grass, Fire and Water.
- **Shauna** (Rival, Route 19): Goodra is bulky: Fairy, Ice or Dragon.
- **Tierno** (Rival, Route 19): Electric covers Talonflame and Crawdaunt.
- **Trevor** (Rival, Route 19): Water or Rock on Aerodactyl; Steel or Poison on Florges.
- **Wulfric** (Gym Leader, Snowbelle Gym): Fire, Fighting, Rock or Steel; Avalugg is very physically bulky.
- **Serena** (Rival, Victory Road): Last rival fight before the League: five Pokémon.
- **Malva** (Elite Four, Pokémon League (Blazing Chamber)): Fire: Water, Rock or Ground.
- **Siebold** (Elite Four, Pokémon League (Flood Chamber)): Water: Electric or Grass.
- **Wikstrom** (Elite Four, Pokémon League (Ironworks Chamber)): Steel: Fire, Fighting or Ground.
- **Drasna** (Elite Four, Pokémon League (Dragonmark Chamber)): Dragon: Fairy or Ice.
- **Diantha** (Champion, Pokémon League (Radiant Chamber)): Mega Gardevoir: Steel or Poison; Ice for Goodra.
- **AZ** (Pokémon Trainer, Lumiose City): Postgame: Torkoal, Golurk and Sigilyph at level 60.
- **Serena** (Rival, Kiloude City): Daily rematch after one Battle Maison battle; Mega Absol: Fighting or Fairy.
- **Essentia** (Looker Bureau, Lysandre Labs): Looker chapter 5: Crobat and Malamar at level 68-70.
