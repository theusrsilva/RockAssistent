## Vaniville Town → Aquacorde Town

**Do**

- Get the Town Map from Mom; Route 1 has no wild Pokémon.
- Pick Chespin, Fennekin or Froakie with Shauna, Tierno and Trevor.
- Shauna takes the starter weak to yours; Calem/Serena takes the one strong against yours.

**Items**

- Town Map (Mom, Vaniville Town).

**Encounters**

:::encounters xy aquacorde-town

**Boss**

:::boss xy shauna-1

- Tip: Any starter move works; she has a single level 5 Pokémon.

## Route 2 → Santalune City

- Get the Roller Skates in Santalune City and challenge Viola.

**Items**

## Camphrier Town → Route 7

- Poké Flute (Furfrou's owner), Sprinklotad (Route 7).
- Mawilite: Shabboneau Castle, Camphrier (postgame, sundial).
