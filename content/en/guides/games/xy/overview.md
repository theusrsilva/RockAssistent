## Version differences

- Box legendary: Xerneas in X, Yveltal in Y.
- Mewtwonite X / Charizardite X only in X; Mewtwonite Y / Charizardite Y only in Y.
- About 20 exclusive Pokémon per version (see Exclusives).

## What's new

- Fairy type; Steel no longer resists Ghost and Dark.
- Mega Evolution (Mega Ring + Mega Stone).
- Horde encounters (5 wild at once) and Sky Battles.
- Exp. Share gives Exp. to the whole party; catching gives Exp.
- Pokémon-Amie, Super Training (EVs) and the Player Search System (PSS).
- Character customization and full 3D.
