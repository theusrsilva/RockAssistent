3. Kiloude City: Battle Maison (Singles, Doubles, Triples, Rotation, Multi) and Friend Safari.

6. Looker Bureau (Rouge Plaza, Lumiose): 5 chapters, ends with Essentia; Diantha's Ralts trade.
7. Legendary bird (by starter) roams Kalos; after 10–15 encounters it waits at Sea Spirit's Den (Azure Bay).
8. Mewtwo in the Unknown Dungeon (Pokémon Village, Surf); Zygarde at the bottom of Terminus Cave.
9. Battle Chateau: higher titles (Duke, Grand Duke) bring Elite Four and Diantha rematches.
