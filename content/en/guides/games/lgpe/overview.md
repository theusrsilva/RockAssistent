## Version differences

- Partner: Pikachu or Eevee, each with its own exclusive partner moves.
- About 10 version-exclusive evolution lines (Oddish × Bellsprout, Growlithe × Vulpix etc.) and different Alolan forms. Details in Exclusives.

## What's new

- Pokémon GO-style catching: no wild battles; every catch gives EXP to the whole team.
- Wild Pokémon visible on the map; catch combos raise IVs, shiny odds and rare spawns.
- Partner Secret Techniques replace HMs; following Pokémon and rideable Pokémon.
- Mega Evolution, AVs (Candies) and Alolan forms via trade.
- No Abilities, no held items, no eggs and no fishing.
- Links with Pokémon GO (GO Park) and the Poké Ball Plus; 2 players on the same screen.
