## After the Hall of Fame

- **Master Trainers:** 153 (one per species, Meltan and Melmetal included), spread across Kanto. 1-on-1 with the same species, no items. Winning gives that species' title.
- **Red:** shows up at the Indigo Plateau entrance after beating 6 Master Trainers. Lv 85 team with Mega Venusaur.
- **Green:** in Cerulean Cave (where Mewtwo was), then returns after each Elite Four win; gives Mewtwonite X and Y.
- **Blue:** new Viridian Gym Leader (lv 66–68, Mega Charizard Y).
- **Elite Four and Trace rematch:** levels 61–67.
- **Mewtwo:** lv 70 at the back of Cerulean Cave (Harjit at the entrance).
- **Daily rematches:** the 7 Gym Leaders, Mina (gives a Bottle Cap) and Morimoto.
- **Kanto sky:** fly high on Charizard, Aerodactyl or Dragonite.
- **Mega Stone shop** at the Indigo Plateau.
- **Game Director** (Celadon Condominiums): Shiny Charm and diploma at 150 species.

## Candies and Lures

| Item | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Candies (Health, Mighty, Tough, Smart, Courage, Quick) | Raise AVs; from sending Pokémon to Oak and from catching |
| Species candy (e.g. Pikachu Candy) | AVs for one species only |
| Lure, Super Lure, Max Lure | More spawns and more rare ones; sold at Poké Marts |
| Golden Razz / Silver Razz / Golden Nanab / Silver Pinap | Stronger catching Berries |
