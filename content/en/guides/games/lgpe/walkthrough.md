## Pallet Town

**Do**
- Get your partner (Pikachu or Eevee) in Oak's lab.
- Battle rival Trace right after.
- Come back with Oak's parcel (Viridian) to get the Pokédex.

**Items:** Town Map (Mom), Razz Berries (Oak).

:::encounters lgpe pallet-town

**Boss**

:::boss lgpe trace-1-lgp

:::boss lgpe trace-1-lge

- Tip: his partner is the opposite of your version; plain attacks do the job.
