## Tips

- **Trace (rival):** uses the opposite partner (Eevee/Jolteon in Pikachu, Pikachu/Raichu in Eevee) + the Pidgey and Oddish lines; Rock and Electric handle Pidgeot.
- **Brock:** Rock/Ground; Grass or Water. Entry needs a Pokémon of one of those types.
- **Misty:** Water; Electric or Grass. Entry needs a Pokémon at lv 15+.
- **Lt. Surge:** Electric; Ground walls the whole team.
- **Erika:** Grass; Fire, Flying, Ice or Poison.
- **Sabrina:** Psychic; Dark, Bug or Ghost. Entry needs a Pokémon at lv 45+.
- **Koga:** Poison; Psychic or Ground. Entry needs 50 species caught.
- **Blaine:** Fire; Water, Rock or Ground. Entry needs the Secret Key and 5 Blaine Dolls.
- **Giovanni (Gym Leader):** Ground; Water, Grass or Ice.
- **Jessie & James / Archer / Giovanni (Rocket):** Poison plus Persian and Rhyhorn; Psychic and Ground.
- **Blue (Silph Co.):** Exeggutor and Charizard; Fire/Flying for the first, Rock/Water for the second.
- **Lorelei:** Water/Ice; Electric, Fighting or Rock.
- **Bruno:** Fighting and Rock; Psychic, Flying and Water.
- **Agatha:** Ghost/Poison; Psychic and Ground.
- **Lance:** Dragon and Flying, with Mega Charizard X; Ice, Rock and Dragon.
- **Trace (Champion):** Mega Pidgeot and a mixed team; bring Electric, Rock, Ice and Ground.
- **Rematches, Blue, Green and Red:** lv 56–85, with Megas; Red bans items.
