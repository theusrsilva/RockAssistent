All bosses in story order, including Kanto, rematches and Red.

:::bosses hgss

## Tips

- **Rival (Cherrygrove)**: a loss does not end the story.
- **Elder Li**: Fire or Flying beats the Bellsprout.
- **Falkner**: Rock or Electric; Pidgeotto heals with Roost.
- **Proton (Slowpoke Well)**: Psychic or Ground moves.
- **Bugsy**: Fire, Flying or Rock against Scyther.
- **Rival (Azalea)**: bring a move that hits Gastly.
- **Whitney**: Fighting (Machop) or Rock against Miltank's Rollout.
- **Rival (Burned Tower)**: Fire or Ground.
- **Morty**: Dark moves; Normal and Fighting do nothing.
- **Eusine**: Dark moves.
- **Chuck**: Flying or Psychic.
- **Jasmine**: Fire, Fighting, Ground or Water on Steelix.
- **Petrel (Hideout)**: any Psychic or Ground move.
- **Ariana (Hideout)**: double battle with Lance; KO Murkrow first.
- **Pryce**: Fire, Fighting or Steel.
- **Petrel (Radio Tower)**: Psychic sweeps the Koffing.
- **Rival (Underground)**: Fighting for Sneasel, Ground for the rest.
- **Proton (Radio Tower)**: Psychic moves (Weezing has Levitate).
- **Ariana (Radio Tower)**: Fire or Flying for Vileplume.
- **Archer**: Water, Fighting or Ground for Houndoom.
- **Clair**: Ice moves; Dragon for Kingdra.
- **Kimono Girls**: five Eeveelutions in a row, no healing; bring varied types.
- **Karate King Kiyo**: Flying or Psychic.
- **Rival (Victory Road)**: Ground and Fighting.
- **Will**: Dark, Ghost or Bug.
- **Koga**: Psychic, Ground or Fire.
- **Bruno**: Flying, Psychic, Water for Onix.
- **Karen**: Fighting or Bug.
- **Lance**: Ice moves; Rock for Charizard.
- **Lt. Surge**: a Ground type.
- **Sabrina**: Dark, Ghost or Bug.
- **Erika**: Fire, Flying, Ice.
- **Janine**: Psychic or Ground.
- **Misty**: Grass and Electric.
- **Brock**: Water and Grass.
- **Rival (Mt. Moon)**: Ground and Fighting.
- **Clair + Lance (Dragon's Den)**: double battle; Ice moves on the dragons.
- **Blaine**: Water and Ground.
- **Blue**: Electric for Gyarados/Pidgeot, Water for Rhydon/Arcanine.
- **Red**: Lv 88 Pikachu; hail all battle; bring Ground and Ice-resistant Pokémon.
- **Rival (Indigo Plateau)**: Rock and Electric for Sneasel and Crobat.
- **Elite Four and Lance rematches**: same types as the first time, Lv 58–75.
- **Gym Leader rematches (Fighting Dojo)**: same main type as their gym, Lv 48–72.
- **Giovanni**: Celebi event only; Water and Ground.
