## Other differences

- Story legendary at Lv 45: Ho-Oh (HG) / Lugia (SS). The other one comes at Lv 70 after the Kanto badges.
- Roaming in Kanto: Latias (HG) / Latios (SS). The other one comes from the Enigma Stone event.
- Embedded Tower orb: Blue Orb (HG, Kyogre) / Red Orb (SS, Groudon).
- Story items: Clear Bell and Rainbow Wing (HG) / Tidal Bell and Silver Wing (SS).
