## Version differences

- Each version has about 15 exclusive Pokémon (see Exclusives).
- HeartGold: Ho-Oh at Lv 45 in the story, Kyogre after Red. SoulSilver: Lugia at Lv 45, Groudon after Red.
- Latias roams Kanto in HeartGold, Latios in SoulSilver.

## What's new

- The first Pokémon in the party walks behind you.
- Pokéwalker: a pedometer accessory that carries one Pokémon and catches items and Pokémon.
- Pokéathlon (Route 35) and Voltorb Flip in the Game Corner.
- Pokégear with phone rematches, map of roaming legendaries and radio stations.
- Battle Frontier (west of Olivine) after the National Dex.
- Gym Leader rematches at the Fighting Dojo in Saffron.
