## Order

1. Beat Lance → credits. Elm gives the S.S. Ticket; Oak gives the National Dex (Olivine port).
2. National Dex unlocks: Battle Frontier, Hoenn/Sinnoh Sound on the radio, more Bug Contest species.
3. Ride the S.S. Aqua to Vermilion (Olivine: Mon and Fri; Vermilion: Wed and Sun).
4. Kanto gyms in any order: Lt. Surge, Sabrina, Erika, Janine, Misty, Brock, Blaine. Viridian (Blue) last.
5. Power Plant: recover the Machine Part (Nugget Bridge Rocket → Cerulean Gym). Magnet Train starts.
6. Lost Item (Fan Club) → Pass. EXPN Card (Lavender) → Poké Flute channel → Snorlax on Route 11.
7. Rival at Mt. Moon; Clair + Lance double battle in the Dragon's Den.
8. Blue on Cinnabar → Seafoam Islands (Blaine) → Viridian Gym (Earth Badge).
9. Oak gives HM08 Rock Climb. Zapdos (Power Plant), Articuno (Seafoam), Mewtwo (Cerulean Cave), Moltres (Mt. Silver).
10. Elite Four with stronger teams.
11. Route 28 → Mt. Silver → Red at the summit.
12. After Red: Kanto and Hoenn starters, Beldum trade, Embedded Tower (Kyogre or Groudon, then Rayquaza).

## Extras

- Battle Frontier: Battle Tower, Factory, Arcade, Castle and Hall. Lv 50, Battle Points (BP).
- Gym Leader rematches at the Fighting Dojo (Saffron), by phone.
- Trainer House (Viridian): one battle per day.
- Pal Park (Fuchsia): bring Pokémon from the GBA games.
- Safari Zone (Johto) and Pokéathlon.
- The other bird at Lv 70: Lugia (HG, Silver Wing) / Ho-Oh (SS, Rainbow Wing).
