Story order. Each stop: what to do, key items, encounters and boss.

## Pallet Town

**Do**

- Take the Potion from your bedroom PC.
- Pick Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle in Oak's Lab.
- Return with Oak's Parcel: get the Pokédex and 5 Poké Balls.
- Ask the rival's sister (Daisy) for the Town Map.

**Items**

- Potion
- Pokédex
- Poké Ball ×5
- Town Map

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb pallet-town

**Boss**

:::boss rb rival-1

- Tip: Right after picking your starter; the rival takes the one with type advantage. Spam Tackle/Scratch and heal with Potion.

## Route 1

**Do**

- Get a free Potion from the Poké Mart clerk.
- Catch Pidgey or Rattata as a second team member.

**Items**

- Potion

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-1

## Viridian City

**Do**

- Pick up Oak's Parcel at the Poké Mart and bring it to Oak.
- After the Pokédex, buy Poké Balls and Antidotes.
- The Gym stays locked until late in the story.

**Items**

- Oak's Parcel
- TM42 Dream Eater (house in town)

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb viridian-city

## Route 22

**Do**

- Route west of Viridian; the League gate checks badges.
- Catch Nidoran♂/♀ and Spearow.
- Keep a Spearow to trade for Farfetch'd in Vermilion.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-22

**Boss**

:::boss rb rival-2

- Tip: Optional on the first visit. Pidgey 9 and his starter 8: heal first.

## Route 2

**Do**

- Head north to Viridian Forest.
- Trade house: Abra → Mr. Mime (later, from the Diglett's Cave side).
- Oak's aide in the gate: HM05 Flash with 10 species caught (needs Cut).

**Items**

- HM05 Flash

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-2

## Viridian Forest

**Do**

- Short maze full of Bug Catchers.
- Pikachu is rare here: good against Misty later.
- Carry Antidotes because of Weedle.

**Items**

- Antidote
- Poké Ball
- Potion

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb viridian-forest

## Pewter City

**Do**

- Visit the museum (₽50 entry).
- Rock Gym: use Grass or Water.
- With Charmander, grind for Ember or use Mankey/Nidoran with Double Kick.

**Items**

- TM34 Bide (Brock)
- Boulder Badge

**Boss**

:::boss rb brock

- Tip: Geodude and Onix: Bubble, Vine Whip or Fighting moves.

## Pewter Museum of Science

**Do**

- Ground floor: fossil exhibit.
- Back annex (with Cut): a scientist gives the Old Amber.

**Items**

- Old Amber

## Route 3

**Do**

- Trainer-packed route; Jigglypuff shows up here.
- In the Pokémon Center by Mt. Moon, a man sells Magikarp for ₽500.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-3

## Mt. Moon

**Do**

- Three-floor cave with Rockets.
- Grab the Moon Stones (one is hidden).
- Beat the Super Nerd and pick the Dome Fossil or Helix Fossil.
- Clefairy is rare on the upper floor.

**Items**

- Moon Stone
- TM12 Water Gun
- TM01 Mega Punch
- Dome Fossil / Helix Fossil

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb mt-moon

## Route 4

**Do**

- Mt. Moon's east exit, one-way ledges to Cerulean.
- Grab TM04 at the end of the route.

**Items**

- TM04 Whirlwind

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-4

## Cerulean City

**Do**

- House next to the Center: trade Poliwhirl → Jynx.
- Bike Shop: the Bicycle costs ₽1,000,000; come back with the Bike Voucher.
- After Bill, enter the robbed house and beat the Rocket: TM28 Dig.
- Water Gym: Grass or Electric.

**Items**

- TM28 Dig
- TM11 BubbleBeam (Misty)
- Cascade Badge

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb cerulean-city

**Bosses**

:::boss rb rival-3

- Tip: At the north exit. Pidgeotto 18, Abra, Rattata and starter 17.

:::boss rb misty

- Tip: Starmie 21 is fast with BubbleBeam: Thundershock/Vine Whip and Potions.

## Nugget Bridge (Route 24)

**Do**

- Beat the 5 bridge trainers: Nugget.
- A Rocket at the end tries to recruit you.
- Catch Oddish/Bellsprout (version-dependent) and Abra.

**Items**

- Nugget
- TM45 Thunder Wave

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb nugget-bridge

## Route 25 / Bill's House

**Do**

- Go to Bill's house (Sea Cottage).
- Help Bill turn back into a human: get the S.S. Ticket.

**Items**

- S.S. Ticket
- TM19 Seismic Toss

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-25

## Route 5

**Do**

- Day Care: a Pokémon gains levels (₽100 + ₽100 per level).
- Take the Underground Path south to Vermilion (Saffron is closed).
- Mankey (Red) or Meowth (Blue) in the grass.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-5

## Underground Path (Routes 5-6)

**Do**

- North–south tunnel, no wild encounters.
- At the north entrance, trade Nidoran♂ → Nidoran♀.

## Route 6

**Do**

- Short trainer route down to Vermilion.
- Catch Oddish/Bellsprout and Pidgey.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-6

## Vermilion City

**Do**

- Pokémon Fan Club: Bike Voucher from the chairman.
- Fishing Guru (house next to the Center): Old Rod.
- Trade Spearow → Farfetch'd.
- Board the S.S. Anne with the S.S. Ticket.

**Items**

- Bike Voucher
- Old Rod

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb vermilion-city

## S.S. Anne

**Do**

- Explore the cabins: TM08 Body Slam and TM44 Rest.
- Rub the seasick captain's back: HM01 Cut.
- The ship leaves once you get off.
- Back in Vermilion, Cut the tree and face Lt. Surge.

**Items**

- HM01 Cut
- TM08 Body Slam
- TM44 Rest

**Bosses**

:::boss rb rival-4

- Tip: In the hallway before the captain's cabin. Raticate and Kadabra; evolved starter at 20.

:::boss rb lt-surge

- Tip: Find the 2 switches in the trash cans. Raichu 24: use Diglett/Dugtrio (Ground).

## Route 11

**Do**

- Gate house: trade Nidorino → Nidorina.
- Oak's aide upstairs in the gate: Itemfinder with 30 species.
- Snorlax blocks the way east until you have the Poké Flute.

**Items**

- Itemfinder

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-11

## Diglett's Cave

**Do**

- Links Vermilion to Route 2.
- Catch Diglett/Dugtrio: great against Lt. Surge.
- On the other side, get HM05 Flash from Oak's aide.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb digletts-cave

## Route 9

**Do**

- Use Cut east of Cerulean.
- Lots of trainers; grab TM30.

**Items**

- TM30 Teleport

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-9

## Route 10

**Do**

- Pokémon Center before Rock Tunnel.
- Voltorb lives here.
- Later, with Surf, the river leads to the Power Plant.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-10

## Rock Tunnel

**Do**

- Dark cave: use Flash.
- Lots of Hikers: bring Water/Grass.
- South exit leads to Lavender Town.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb rock-tunnel

## Lavender Town

**Do**

- The Name Rater changes nicknames.
- Pokémon Tower can only be cleared with the Silph Scope.
- Head west to Celadon via Route 8.

## Pokémon Tower

**Do**

- Without the Silph Scope, ghosts can't be fought.
- With the Silph Scope, face the Marowak ghost (can't be caught).
- Rescue Mr. Fuji at the top: Poké Flute.

**Items**

- Poké Flute

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb pokemon-tower

**Boss**

:::boss rb rival-5

- Tip: On 2F. Five Pokémon at 20–25; bring an Electric or Grass type.

## Route 8

**Do**

- The Saffron gate guard wants a drink.
- Growlithe (Red) or Vulpix (Blue) appears here.
- Use the Underground Path to reach Celadon.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-8

## Underground Path (Routes 7-8)

**Do**

- East–west tunnel, no wild encounters.
- Use the Itemfinder: hidden items here.

## Route 7

**Do**

- Short route between Saffron and Celadon.
- Growlithe/Vulpix and Oddish/Bellsprout.

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-7

## Celadon City

**Do**

- Get the Coin Case from the man in the restaurant.
- Grass Gym (needs Cut): Fire, Ice or Flying.
- TM41 Soft-Boiled from a man in town.
- Buy drinks at the Dept. Store for the Saffron guard.

**Items**

- Coin Case
- TM41 Soft-Boiled
- TM21 Mega Drain (Erika)
- Rainbow Badge

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb celadon-city

## Celadon Mansion

**Do**

- Back entrance → top floor: free Eevee Lv. 25.
- Game Freak room: the diploma for completing the Pokédex.

**Items**

- Eevee

## Celadon Dept. Store

**Do**

- 2F: TMs for sale (01, 02, 05, 07, 09, 17, 32, 33, 37).
- 4F: evolution stones and Poké Doll.
- Roof: give Fresh Water, Soda Pop and Lemonade to the girl for TM13, TM48 and TM49.
- 3F: TM18 Counter as a gift.

**Items**

- TM13 Ice Beam
- TM18 Counter
- TM48 Rock Slide
- TM49 Tri Attack
- Fire / Water / Thunder / Leaf Stone

## Game Corner

**Do**

- Win coins at the slots (needs the Coin Case).
- Prizes: Pokémon, TM15, TM23 and TM50.
- Beat the Rocket by the poster and press the switch: opens the hideout.

**Items**

- TM15 Hyper Beam
- TM23 Dragon Rage
- TM50 Substitute

**Boss**

:::boss rb erika

- Tip: Victreebel and Vileplume 29 use Sleep Powder: bring Awakenings and use Fire/Flying.

## Rocket Hideout

**Do**

- Spinner tiles on B2F–B4F.
- Beat the Rocket holding the Lift Key (B4F).
- Beat Giovanni: Silph Scope.

**Items**

- Lift Key
- Silph Scope
- TM02 Razor Wind
- TM07 Horn Drill
- TM10 Double-Edge

**Boss**

:::boss rb giovanni-hideout

- Tip: Onix, Rhyhorn and Kangaskhan 29: Water or Grass handles the first two.

## Route 16

**Do**

- Wake Snorlax (Lv. 30) with the Poké Flute.
- Hidden house (Cut): HM02 Fly.
- Leads to Cycling Road (needs the Bicycle).

**Items**

- HM02 Fly

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb route-16

## Saffron City

**Do**

- Give a drink to any gate guard to enter.
- Mr. Psychic: TM29 Psychic.
- Copycat: give a Poké Doll for TM31 Mimic.
- Silph Co. is taken over by Team Rocket.

**Items**

- TM29 Psychic
- TM31 Mimic

**Encounters**

:::encounters rb saffron-city

## Silph Co.

**Do**

- Get the Card Key on 5F to open the doors.
- Use the warp tiles; on 7F, get Lapras Lv. 15.
- Beat Giovanni on 11F: the president gives the Master Ball.

**Items**

- Card Key
- Lapras
- Master Ball
- TM03 Swords Dance
- TM09 Take Down
- TM26 Earthquake
- TM36 Self-Destruct

**Bosses**

:::boss rb rival-6

- Tip: On 7F. Starter Lv. 40 and Alakazam: heal before stepping in.

:::boss rb giovanni-silph

- Tip: Nidoqueen 41 and Nidorino: Water/Ice/Psychic.

## Fighting Dojo

**Do**

- Beat the Karate Master.
- Choose Hitmonlee or Hitmonchan (Lv. 30).
- Then the Gym next door (Sabrina) with warp tiles.

**Items**

- Hitmonlee / Hitmonchan
- TM46 Psywave (Sabrina)
- Marsh Badge

**Boss**

:::boss rb sabrina

- Tip: Alakazam 43 hits hard; Psychic has no useful weakness here. Use strong physical moves (Body Slam, Earthquake).

- Deepest area: Secret House with HM03 Surf.
- Find the Gold Teeth for the Warden.

## Indigo Plateau

**Do**

- Buy Full Restores and Revives at the Poké Mart.
- Five battles in a row, no Center in between.
- Recommended level: 55–65.

**Bosses**

:::boss rb lorelei

- Tip: Ice/Water: Electric and Fighting.

:::boss rb bruno

- Tip: Fighting/Rock: Psychic, Water and Flying.

:::boss rb agatha

- Tip: Ghost/Poison: Psychic and Ground (Ghost moves don't hit Psychic in this gen).

:::boss rb lance

- Tip: Dragons and Gyarados: Ice (Blizzard/Ice Beam) and Electric.

:::boss rb blue-champion

- Tip: Mixed team based on your starter; carry Electric, Ice and Water coverage.

**Do**

- Postgame: Surf west of Nugget Bridge (Cerulean).
- Wild Pokémon Lv. 46–67.
- Mewtwo Lv. 70 on the lowest floor: use the Master Ball.
