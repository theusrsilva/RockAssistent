## Tips

- **Blue** (Rival, Oak's Lab, Pallet Town): Right after picking your starter; the rival takes the one with type advantage. Spam Tackle/Scratch and heal with Potion.
- **Blue** (Rival, Route 22): Optional on the first visit. Pidgey 9 and his starter 8: heal first.
- **Brock** (Gym Leader, Pewter Gym): Geodude and Onix: Bubble, Vine Whip or Fighting moves.
- **Blue** (Rival, Cerulean City (north exit, before Nugget Bridge)): At the north exit. Pidgeotto 18, Abra, Rattata and starter 17.
- **Misty** (Gym Leader, Cerulean Gym): Starmie 21 is fast with BubbleBeam: Thundershock/Vine Whip and Potions.
- **Blue** (Rival, S.S. Anne 2F): In the hallway before the captain's cabin. Raticate and Kadabra; evolved starter at 20.
- **Lt. Surge** (Gym Leader, Vermilion Gym): Find the 2 switches in the trash cans. Raichu 24: use Diglett/Dugtrio (Ground).
- **Erika** (Gym Leader, Celadon Gym): Victreebel and Vileplume 29 use Sleep Powder: bring Awakenings and use Fire/Flying.
- **Giovanni** (Team Rocket Boss, Rocket Hideout B4F): Onix, Rhyhorn and Kangaskhan 29: Water or Grass handles the first two.
- **Blue** (Rival, Pokémon Tower 2F): On 2F. Five Pokémon at 20–25; bring an Electric or Grass type.
- **Blue** (Rival, Silph Co. 7F): On 7F. Starter Lv. 40 and Alakazam: heal before stepping in.
- **Giovanni** (Team Rocket Boss, Silph Co. 11F): Nidoqueen 41 and Nidorino: Water/Ice/Psychic.
- **Koga** (Gym Leader, Fuchsia Gym): Weezing 43 has Self-Destruct: use Psychic/Ground and cure poison.
- **Sabrina** (Gym Leader, Saffron Gym): Alakazam 43 hits hard; Psychic has no useful weakness here. Use strong physical moves (Body Slam, Earthquake).
- **Blaine** (Gym Leader, Cinnabar Gym): Arcanine 47: Water or Ground. Answering the quiz skips trainers.
- **Giovanni** (Gym Leader, Viridian Gym): Ground team: Water, Grass and Ice. Rhydon 50 is the strongest.
- **Blue** (Rival, Route 22): On Route 22. Starter Lv. 53: fully heal first.
- **Lorelei** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Ice/Water: Electric and Fighting.
- **Bruno** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Fighting/Rock: Psychic, Water and Flying.
- **Agatha** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Ghost/Poison: Psychic and Ground (Ghost moves don't hit Psychic in this gen).
- **Lance** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Dragons and Gyarados: Ice (Blizzard/Ice Beam) and Electric.
- **Blue** (Champion, Indigo Plateau): Mixed team based on your starter; carry Electric, Ice and Water coverage.
