## Red only

- Ekans, Arbok, Oddish, Gloom, Vileplume, Mankey, Primeape, Growlithe, Arcanine, Scyther, Electabuzz

## Blue only

- Sandshrew, Sandslash, Vulpix, Ninetales, Meowth, Persian, Bellsprout, Weepinbell, Victreebel, Magmar, Pinsir

## How to complete

- Trade with the other version (or Yellow) via Link Cable.
- Game Corner: Red has Scyther and Nidorina; Blue has Pinsir and Nidorino.
- Mew: events only.
