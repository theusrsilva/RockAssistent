- Anime-inspired: Pikachu walks behind you and shows its mood.
- Jessie and James show up as bosses.
- Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle are gifted during the story.
- Leader teams changed (e.g. Lt. Surge only has Raichu).
- Pikachu's Beach minigame (via Pokémon Stadium) for a Pikachu with Surf.
