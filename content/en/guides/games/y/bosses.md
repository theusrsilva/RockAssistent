## Tips

- **Blue** (Rival, Oak's Lab, Pallet Town): Eevee Lv. 5. Pikachu only has ThunderShock and Growl: tank it with Potions.
- **Blue** (Rival, Route 22): Spearow 9 and Eevee 8: Pikachu beats Spearow easily.
- **Brock** (Gym Leader, Pewter Gym): Pikachu is useless here. Use Mankey (Low Kick), Nidoran♂ (Double Kick) or Butterfree (Confusion).
- **Jessie & James** (Team Rocket, Mt. Moon B2F): On B2F, before the fossils. Ekans, Meowth and Koffing Lv. 14.
- **Blue** (Rival, Cerulean City (north exit, before Nugget Bridge)): Spearow 18, Sandshrew, Rattata and Eevee 17.
- **Misty** (Gym Leader, Cerulean Gym): Pikachu and Bulbasaur handle Starmie 21.
- **Blue** (Rival, S.S. Anne 2F): His starter is Eevee 20; plus Spearow, Sandshrew and Rattata.
- **Lt. Surge** (Gym Leader, Vermilion Gym): A single Raichu 28: Ground (Diglett/Dugtrio) or Sandshrew.
- **Erika** (Gym Leader, Celadon Gym): Tangela, Weepinbell and Gloom: Fire or Flying; watch for Sleep Powder.
- **Jessie & James** (Team Rocket, Rocket Hideout B4F): On B4F, before Giovanni. Arbok, Weezing and Meowth 25.
- **Giovanni** (Team Rocket Boss, Rocket Hideout B4F): Onix, Rhyhorn and Persian 29: Water/Grass.
- **Blue** (Rival, Pokémon Tower 2F): On 2F. Eevee has evolved based on your early choices.
- **Jessie & James** (Team Rocket, Pokémon Tower 7F): On 7F, before Mr. Fuji. Arbok, Weezing and Meowth 27.
- **Blue** (Rival, Silph Co. 7F): On 7F. Eevee's evolution at Lv. 40.
- **Jessie & James** (Team Rocket, Silph Co. 11F): On 11F, before Giovanni. Arbok, Weezing and Meowth 31.
- **Giovanni** (Team Rocket Boss, Silph Co. 11F): Nidoqueen 41, Nidorino and Persian: Water/Ice/Psychic.
- **Koga** (Gym Leader, Fuchsia Gym): Three Venonat and Venomoth 50: Fire, Psychic or Flying.
- **Sabrina** (Gym Leader, Saffron Gym): Abra, Kadabra and Alakazam 50: strong physical moves; Ghost doesn't help this gen.
- **Blaine** (Gym Leader, Cinnabar Gym): Ninetales, Rapidash and Arcanine 54: Water or Ground.
- **Giovanni** (Gym Leader, Viridian Gym): Ground team Lv. 50–55: Water, Grass and Ice.
- **Blue** (Rival, Route 22): On Route 22. Eevee's evolution at Lv. 53.
- **Lorelei** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Ice/Water: Electric and Fighting.
- **Bruno** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Fighting/Rock: Psychic, Water and Flying.
- **Agatha** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Ghost/Poison: Psychic and Ground (Ghost moves don't hit Psychic in this gen).
- **Lance** (Elite Four, Indigo Plateau): Dragons and Gyarados: Ice (Blizzard/Ice Beam) and Electric.
- **Blue** (Champion, Indigo Plateau): Eevee evolution Lv. 65 and a mixed team: carry Electric, Ice and Ground.
