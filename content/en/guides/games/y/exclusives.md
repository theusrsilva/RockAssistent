## Missing from Yellow (trade only)

- Weedle, Kakuna, Beedrill
- Ekans, Arbok, Meowth, Persian, Koffing, Weezing
- Raichu (the starter Pikachu refuses to evolve; another Pikachu can, with a Thunder Stone)
- Jynx, Electabuzz, Magmar
- Mew (event)

## Differences from Red/Blue

- All three starters are gifts: Bulbasaur (Cerulean), Charmander (Route 24), Squirtle (Vermilion).
- Farfetch'd is wild (Routes 12 and 13), no trade needed.
- Game Corner prizes: Abra, Vulpix, Wigglytuff, Scyther, Pinsir, Porygon.

## How to complete

- Trade with Red/Blue via Link Cable.
