- **Mystery Gift** (infrared): unlocked on Goldenrod Dept. Store 5F; up to 5 exchanges per day, one per partner, items and decorations.
- **GS Ball**: Mobile System event in Japan; on the 3DS Virtual Console (2018) everyone gets it after the Hall of Fame (Goldenrod Pokémon Center).
- Take the GS Ball to Kurt, then to the Ilex Forest shrine: **Celebi Lv. 30**.
- **Mobile System GB** (Japan only): online Battle Tower, Trade Corner and news.
- **Mew**: Gen 1 only, via the Time Capsule.
