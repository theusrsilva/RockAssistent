## Missing from Crystal (trade only)

- Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle and evolutions
- Vulpix, Ninetales, Mankey, Primeape
- Mareep, Flaaffy, Ampharos, Girafarig, Remoraid, Octillery
- Omanyte, Omastar, Kabuto, Kabutops
- Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Mewtwo, Mew

## Differences from Gold/Silver

- Ho-Oh and Lugia at Lv. 60; static Suicune at Tin Tower (Lv. 40).
- Game Corner prizes: Goldenrod (Abra, Cubone, Wobbuffet) and Celadon (Pikachu, Porygon, Larvitar).
- New trades: Dodrio, Xatu and Magneton.

## How to complete

- Trade with Gold/Silver via Link Cable; Gen 1 via the Time Capsule.
