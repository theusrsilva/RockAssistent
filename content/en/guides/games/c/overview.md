## What's new

- First time you can play as a girl.
- Animated sprites at the start of battle.
- Suicune storyline with Eusine; static Suicune at Tin Tower.
- Battle Tower near Olivine; Move Tutor in Goldenrod.
- Odd Egg, Dratini with ExtremeSpeed and Ruins of Alph changes.
- Mobile System GB (Japan only): online battles and trades.
