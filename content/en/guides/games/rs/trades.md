## Gifts

- Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip Lv. 5: Prof. Birch's bag, Route 101.
- Wynaut Egg: hot springs old lady, Lavaridge (hatches at Lv. 5).
- Castform Lv. 25: Weather Institute.
- Beldum Lv. 5: Steven's house, Mossdeep (postgame).

## Fossils

- Root Fossil (Lileep) or Claw Fossil (Anorith): Route 111 desert, pick one.
- Revive at Devon Corporation (Rustboro): Lv. 20.

## Eggs

- Day Care on Route 117: 2 compatible Pokémon make Eggs.
