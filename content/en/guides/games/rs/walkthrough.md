Story order. Villains: Team Magma in Ruby, Team Aqua in Sapphire. Each stop: what to do, items, encounters and boss.

## Littleroot Town

**Do**

- Set the clock in your room (Berries and tides depend on it).
- Visit the rival's house and Prof. Birch's lab.
- After the rival, get the Pokédex and 5 Poké Balls; Mom gives the Running Shoes.

**Items**

- Pokédex
- Poké Ball ×5
- Running Shoes

## Route 101

**Do**

- Save Prof. Birch from the Poochyena: pick Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip from his bag.
- Zigzagoon and Wurmple live here.

**Encounters**

**Do**

- The west path is blocked until you have the Pokédex.

**Items**
