## Ruby only

- Seedot, Nuzleaf, Shiftry
- Mawile, Zangoose, Solrock
- Groudon; Latios (roaming)

## Sapphire only

- Lotad, Lombre, Ludicolo
- Sableye, Seviper, Lunatone
- Kyogre; Latias (roaming)

## How to complete

- Trade with the other version, Emerald or FireRed/LeafGreen (the Wireless Adapter doesn't work with RS; use a cable).
- The missing Latios/Latias comes from Southern Island with the Eon Ticket (event).
- Kanto/Johto Pokémon: trade with FireRed/LeafGreen or via Pokémon Colosseum.
