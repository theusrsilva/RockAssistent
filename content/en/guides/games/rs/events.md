- **Eon Ticket**: opens Southern Island (Latias in Ruby, Latios in Sapphire, Lv. 50). Distributed via e-Reader (Japan and US, Nintendo Power issue 173 card, 2003) and at events in Europe; in Emerald it only arrives by Record Mixing.
- **Jirachi**: came on the Pokémon Colosseum bonus disc (US/Canada, 2003 pre-order) and in Pokémon Channel (Europe and Australia).
- **Mew, Celebi, Deoxys**: official event distributions.
