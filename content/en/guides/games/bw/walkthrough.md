- Pick Snivy, Tepig or Oshawott in your room.
- Double battle in a row: Bianca, then Cheren.
- Visit Professor Juniper on Route 1 (Pokédex).

- Xtransceiver and Town Map (mom)

## Striaton City → Dreamyard

**Do**

- Trainers' School: Cheren fights you; take the quiz for a Full Heal.
- Get the elemental monkey from the girl in the Dreamyard (Pansage, Pansear or Panpour: the one that beats the leader).
- Beat the leader matching your starter: Chili (Snivy), Cress (Tepig) or Cilan (Oshawott). Trio Badge.
- Meet Fennel in the Dreamyard; she gives the C-Gear.

**Items**

- HM01 Cut (Fennel), Dusk Ball and Great Ball (Striaton)
- TM83 Work Up (Striaton Gym prize), TM85 Dream Eater (Dreamyard)

**Encounters**

:::encounters bw dreamyard

**Boss**

:::boss bw cheren-2

- Tip: Purrloin and his starter at Lv 8.

:::boss bw striaton-trio

- Tip: the monkey from the Dreamyard wins this fight.
