Every boss in story order. Rival teams change with your starter (see each card).

## Tips

- **Bianca and Cheren (Nuvema)**: two Lv 5 fights in a row; just use your starter's basic attack.
- **N (Accumula)**: lone Purrloin Lv 7; any starter wins.
- **Bianca (Route 2)**: Lillipup first; heal between fights.
- **Cheren (Striaton school)**: Purrloin and his starter, both Lv 8.
- **Cilan / Chili / Cress (Striaton)**: use the elemental monkey from the Dreamyard that beats the leader's monkey; Work Up from Lillipup hurts.
- **Cheren (Route 3)**: Purrloin uses Sand-Attack; switch if accuracy drops.
- **N (Nacrene)**: Pidove, Tympole and Timburr at Lv 13.
- **Lenora (Nacrene)**: Watchog's Retaliate hits hard after a faint; Fighting types (Timburr, Sawk, Throh) shine.
- **Burgh (Castelia)**: Fire and Flying (Pidove/Tranquill, Pansear, Tepig line) win; beware Leavanny's Razor Leaf.
- **Bianca (Route 4)**: four Pokémon around Lv 18-20.
- **Cheren (Route 4)**: four Pokémon around Lv 20-22.
- **N (Nimbasa)**: Sandile, Darumaka, Scraggy, Sigilyph at Lv 22.
- **Elesa (Nimbasa)**: Ground types (Sandile, Drilbur) are immune to Volt Switch; Emolga is Flying, so use Rock or Ice on it.
- **Cheren (Route 5)**: four Pokémon at Lv 24-26.
- **Clay (Driftveil)**: Water and Grass; Excadrill has Mold Breaker and Rock Slide.
- **Bianca (Route 6)**: Musharna is bulky; bring a Bug or Dark move.
- **N (Chargestone Cave)**: Boldore, Ferroseed, Joltik and Klink; Fire handles three of them.
- **Skyla (Mistralton)**: Electric and Rock (Galvantula, Boldore); Swanna resists Fire and Ground.
- **Cheren (Route 7)**: Lv 33-35 with Unfezant.
- **Brycen (Icirrus)**: Fire, Fighting and Steel; Beartic is slow but strong.
- **Bianca (Route 8)**: her starter is fully evolved now.
- **Drayden / Iris (Opelucid)**: Ice (Cubchoo, Vanillite, Ice Beam) or Dragon; Haxorus is fast.
- **Cheren (Route 10)**: last story fight, Lv 43-45.
- **Shauntal**: Dark moves; Chandelure is the main threat.
- **Grimsley**: Fighting and Bug; Bisharp is weak to Fighting and Ground.
- **Caitlin**: Bug, Dark and Ghost; Gothitelle with Shadow Ball or Crunch.
- **Marshal**: Flying and Psychic; Sigilyph, Swoobat or Unfezant help.
- **N (N's Castle)**: your Reshiram/Zekrom joins you; use Dragon moves on his legendary, the rest is mixed.
- **Ghetsis**: Hydreigon is weak to Fighting, Bug, Dragon and Ice.
- **Elite Four rematches**: postgame, Lv 71-73, six Pokémon each.
- **Alder (Champion)**: postgame; Volcarona fears Rock (4x).
- **Bianca (rematch)**: Saturdays in Juniper's Lab.
- **Cheren (rematch)**: Victory Road, postgame.
- **Cynthia**: Undella Town, spring and summer only; Garchomp Lv 77.
- **Morimoto**: Game Freak building, Castelia; once a day.
