## Version differences

- Black: Reshiram on the cover, Tornadus, Black City, Drayden in Opelucid, Rotation Battles.
- White: Zekrom on the cover, Thundurus, White Forest, Iris in Opelucid, Triple Battles.
- Full list in Exclusives.

## What's new

- Only new Pokémon (156) until the Hall of Fame; older ones appear in the postgame.
- Seasons change every month and alter routes, encounters and Deerling/Sawsbuck forms.
- Triple and Rotation Battles; shaking grass, dust clouds and dark grass (wild double battles).
- TMs are reusable; Flash and Rock Smash became TMs.
- C-Gear and Entralink, Pokémon Musical, Battle Subway and Dream World (Hidden Abilities).
