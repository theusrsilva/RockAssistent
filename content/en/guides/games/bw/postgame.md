1. Beat N and Ghetsis; credits roll and you wake at home.
2. Looker gives the Super Rod; Cedric Juniper gives the National Dex.
3. Find the six Seven Sages (any order): Rood, Zinzolin (Cold Storage), Bronius (Chargestone Cave), Gorm (Dreamyard), Ryoku (Relic Castle), Giallo (Route 14). Each gives a TM.
4. Relic Castle new rooms: Volcarona Lv 70 and TM26 Earthquake.
5. Challenger's Cave (south of Route 9) and the Royal Unova cruise (Castelia).
6. East Unova: Route 11, Village Bridge, Route 12, Lacunosa Town, Route 13.
7. Giant Chasm: Kyurem Lv 75.
8. Undella Town: HM06 Dive, Cynthia (spring and summer), Abyssal Ruins.
9. Route 14 and Abundant Shrine: Landorus (with Tornadus and Thundurus).
10. Black City or White Forest; Route 15 (PokéTransfer Lab, Ditto for Rotom); Marvelous Bridge (Orbs).
11. Strong trainers: Bianca (Saturdays), Cheren (Victory Road), Morimoto (Castelia).
12. Rematch the stronger Elite Four, then Champion Alder for the real Hall of Fame.

## Facilities

- Battle Subway (Nimbasa): 8 lines, 7 battles per run, Super lines after the bosses.
- Battle Institute (Nimbasa): 5 battles, scored.
- PokéTransfer Lab (Route 15): moves Pokémon from DP, Pt and HGSS (no items, no HMs).
