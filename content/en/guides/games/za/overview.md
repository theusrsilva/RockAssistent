## Version differences

- Single version: no exclusive Pokémon. The Switch 2 Edition only improves resolution and frame rate.
- The rival (Urbain or Taunie) and their starters depend on your look and your starter.

## What's new

- Real-time battles, with per-move cooldowns and Plus Moves.
- Mega Evolution powered by Mega Power gathered in battle; Rogue Mega Evolutions as bosses.
- Battle Zones at night (Ticket Points) and Wild Zones by day; 48-minute real-time cycle.
- New Megas in the base game (Mega Floette, Mega Zygarde, Mega Dragalge, Mega Starmie...) and 22 more forms in the DLC.
