## After the credits

- **Wild Zone 20** (Prism Tower ruins): all 6 starters and Alphas of almost the whole Pokédex.
- **Infinite Z-A Royale** at Quasartico Inc.: reward matches 1–20 in fixed order; from 21 on, random picks among all (+4 levels), plus Josée, Asami and Andi.
- **Zach** is reward 1; reward 15 (rival with Mega Floette) gives the Eternal Flower Floette and the Floettite.
- **IV Judge** unlocked (MM38). AZ's Lucario at Hotel Z.
- **Remaining starter Mega Stones** for sale at the Stone Emporium.
- **Legendaries:** Xerneas (MM40), Yveltal (MM41), L and Zygarde (MM42). See the Legendaries tab.
- **New Side Missions:** Raichu → Alolan Raichu (SM108), Jacinthe ×4 (SM119), Diancie and Mewtwo with the Mystery Gift stones.

## Mega Dimension (DLC)

- Starts in Lumiose when Hoopa's rings appear; donut chef Ansha leads the way to Hyperspace Lumiose, with Korrina.
- Hyperspace wild Pokémon go above lv 100; Ansha's donuts (Flavor Powers) give boosts.
- Hyperspace Missions, portals with Mega Pokémon and Legendaries from other regions (see Legendaries).
- 22 new Mega forms from 18 species: Raichu X/Y, Chimecho, Absol Z, Staraptor, Garchomp Z, Lucario Z, Heatran, Darkrai, Golurk, Meowstic, Crabominable, Golisopod, Magearna (two colors), Zeraora, Scovillain, Glimmora, Tatsugiri (three forms), Baxcalibur.
