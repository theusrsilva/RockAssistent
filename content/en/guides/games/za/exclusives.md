## Versions

Legends: Z-A has a single version: no exclusive Pokémon.

| Edition | Difference |
| --- | --- |
| Nintendo Switch | Full game |
| Nintendo Switch 2 Edition | Same content, with higher resolution and frame rate |

## Choices that change the game

| Choice | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Protagonist look | The rival is the other protagonist: Urbain or Taunie |
| Chikorita starter | Rival ends with Feraligatr and Mega Emboar |
| Tepig starter | Rival ends with Meganium and Mega Feraligatr |
| Totodile starter | Rival ends with Emboar and Mega Meganium |
| Chosen starter | AZ gives its Mega Stone (MM10); the other two are sold postgame (Stone Emporium) |

## Paid content

| Content | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| Mega Dimension (DLC) | Hyperspace Lumiose, Hoopa, 22 new Mega forms, Legendaries from other regions, TM108–TM160 |
