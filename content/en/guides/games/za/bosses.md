## Tips

- **Zach (Rank Z):** Pikachu is the ace; Ground or Grass hold well.
- **Yvon (Rank Y):** Fairy; use Poison, Steel or Fire.
- **Xavi (Rank X):** Poison/Psychic; Kadabra falls to Dark, Bug or Ghost.
- **Rintaro (Rank W):** the three elemental monkeys; bring Flying or a mixed team.
- **Vinnie (Rank V):** Mega Drampa; Fighting and Fairy.
- **Canari (Rank F, Super-Tournoi):** Electric; Ground, except on Eelektross.
- **Ivor (Rank E, Super-Tournoi):** Fighting (Mega Falinks); Flying, Psychic, Fairy.
- **Corbeau (Rank D, Super-Tournoi):** Poison with Mega Scolipede; Psychic and Ground (Fire/Flying on Scolipede).
- **Jacinthe (Rank C):** Fairy (Mega Clefable); Steel and Poison.
- **Grisham (Rank B):** mixed team; Mega Charizard X falls to Ground, Rock and Dragon.
- **Rival (Urbain/Taunie):** 3 battles; the team follows the starters you didn't pick; the last one has a lv 64 Mega starter.
- **Naveen and Lida (Team MZ):** Naveen is Dark/Bug (Mega Scrafty: Fairy, Flying); Lida is Water/Ice (Electric).
- **Philippe (Rust Syndicate):** Steel (Mega Skarmory); Fire and Electric.
- **Gwynn:** Ghost (Mega Chandelure); Dark, Water, Ground.
- **Griselle (Team Flare Nouveau):** Mega Pyroar; Water, Ground, Rock.
- **Rogue Megas:** worth their Mega Stone; watch their big attacks and dodge.
- **L (postgame):** lv 78–80, Mega Gyarados; Electric and Rock.
