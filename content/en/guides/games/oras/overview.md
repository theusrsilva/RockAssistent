## Version differences

- Villain team: Team Magma and Groudon in OR; Team Aqua and Kyogre in AS.
- Latios joins you in OR, Latias in AS.
- Mirage Spot legendaries differ (Ho-Oh/Lugia, Palkia/Dialga, Reshiram/Zekrom, Tornadus/Thundurus).

## What's new

- Primal Reversion (Groudon/Kyogre) and 20 new Mega Evolutions.
- DexNav: sneak up on hidden Pokémon with egg moves or Hidden Abilities.
- Soaring on Mega Latios/Latias and daily Mirage Spots.
- Delta Episode after the League (Rayquaza and Deoxys).
- Contest Spectacular and Super Secret Bases.
