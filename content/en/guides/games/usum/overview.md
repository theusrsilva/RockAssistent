## Version differences

- **Ultra Sun:** Solgaleo and Dusk Mane Necrozma; Buzzwole, Kartana and Blacephalon; Totem Gumshoos.
- **Ultra Moon:** Lunala and Dawn Wings Necrozma; Pheromosa, Celesteela and Stakataka; Totem Alolan Raticate.
- Different legendaries in the Ultra Wormhole and in Episode RR (details in Exclusives).

## What's new

- New story with the Ultra Recon Squad and Ultra Necrozma (Megalopolis).
- Ultra Warp Ride: legendaries from Gens 1–6 and Ultra Beasts.
- Episode RR (Team Rainbow Rocket), Mantine Surf, Alola Photo Club, Battle Agency.
- Bigger Alola Pokédex (403 species), new Totems and exclusive Z-Moves.
