## Tips

- **Hau (rival):** always picks the starter that beats yours; later adds Alolan Raichu, Noivern, Tauros and Crabominable.
- **Ilima (Totem Gumshoos/Raticate):** Normal; Fighting.
- **Hala:** Fighting; Flying or Psychic.
- **Lana (Totem Araquanid):** Water/Bug; Electric, Flying, Rock.
- **Kiawe (Totem Alolan Marowak):** Fire/Ghost; Water, Ground, Dark.
- **Mallow (Totem Lurantis):** Grass under sun; Fire, Flying.
- **Olivia:** Rock; Water, Grass, Fighting.
- **Sophocles (Totem Togedemaru):** Electric/Steel; Ground.
- **Acerola (Totem Mimikyu):** Ghost/Fairy with Disguise; Steel, Ghost.
- **Nanu:** Dark; Fighting, Bug, Fairy.
- **Totem Kommo-o:** Dragon/Fighting; Fairy, Flying, Psychic.
- **Mina (Totem Ribombee):** Bug/Fairy; Fire, Flying, Steel, Rock.
- **Hapu:** Ground; Water, Grass, Ice.
- **Gladion:** Silvally changes type to beat your starter; have an answer.
- **Team Skull (Plumeria, Guzma):** Poison/Fire and Bug; Ground, Psychic, Fire, Flying.
- **Aether (Faba, Lusamine):** Faba is Psychic (Dark, Ghost); Lusamine mixes types.
- **Necrozma / Ultra Necrozma:** Psychic; Ultra Necrozma (Psychic/Dragon) is the toughest story boss; Dark, Ghost, Fairy, Ice.
- **Elite Four:** Molayne (Steel), Olivia (Rock), Acerola (Ghost), Kahili (Flying).
- **Team Rainbow Rocket:** Archie, Maxie, Cyrus, Lysandre, Ghetsis and Giovanni close with a legendary (levels 66–70).
- **Title defense:** varied challengers at levels 66–70; Kukui and Hau have the strongest teams.
