## First steps

- Catch Solgaleo (US) or Lunala (UM) at the Plank Bridge if you haven't yet.
- Poni Grove: the Ultra Recon Squad gives 5 Beast Balls for Blacephalon (US) / Stakataka (UM).
- Kukui gives a Lucky Egg (after 50 catches); Hala gives the other starters' Z-Crystals.
- Captain and Totem rematches; Morimoto and Iwao at Game Freak (Oval Charm; Shiny Charm with the full Alola Dex).

## Ultra Beasts and Ultra Wormhole

- Ultra Warp Ride from the Altar: white wormholes lead to Ultra Beasts (lv 60); colored wormholes lead to older legendaries.
- The farther and rarer the wormhole, the higher the shiny chance for regular Pokémon.
- Beast Balls are sold at Aether Paradise.
- Full tables in Legendaries.

## Title defense

- Beat the Elite Four again (lv 66): a random challenger shows up at the end.
- Challengers: Hau, Gladion, Lusamine, Guzma, Plumeria, Sophocles, Hapu, Ryuki, Tristan and Kukui.
- Gladion and Lusamine have special date conditions (to be confirmed).

## Battle Tree

- Entrance at the Poni Gauntlet; first beat Red or Blue.
- Single, Double and Multi; everything at level 50; no repeated species or items.
- Streak rewards: 20 Rare Candy, 50 Ability Capsule, 200 Starf Berry; BP buy items and Mega Stones.
- Battle Legends (Red and Blue) on the 20th and 50th battle.

## Battle Agency

- Festival Plaza: build a team of rental Pokémon and win 3 battles in a row.
- Needed for the Team Rocket takeover of Festival Plaza.

## Island Scan

- QR Scanner: with 100 points, run an Island Scan and find, for 1 hour, a Pokémon that isn't in the regular Alola Dex.
- The Pokémon changes by island and day of the week.

## Mantine Surf

- Routes between islands; tricks earn BP.
- High scores on all 4 courses unlock the Pikachu with Surf (Heahea City).

## Alola Photo Club

- Hau'oli City: take photos with backgrounds and poses; new sets appear as you progress.

## Other

- Dexio: Key Stone and Alakazite (Poni Meadow) and Zygarde 10% with the Zygarde Cube (Route 16).
- Zygarde 50% in Resolution Cave; Zygarde Cells spread across Alola.
- Aristo on Mount Lanakila after beating every area's lead trainer.
- Eeveelution trainers (lv 55) and Eevium Z.
- Mr. Hyper (Hyper Training with Bottle Caps) at the Hau'oli Mall.
