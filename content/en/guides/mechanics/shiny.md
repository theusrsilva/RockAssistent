## Base odds

| Generations | Odds | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gen 2 | 1/8192 | Set by DVs; a shiny parent passes better odds to its babies |
| Gen 3–5 | 1/8192 | Set by the PID and the trainer ID |
| Gen 6+ | 1/4096 | |

- First guaranteed shiny: the Red Gyarados at the Lake of Rage (GSC, HGSS).
- Each extra "roll" raises the odds: 3 rolls at 1/4096 ≈ 1/1365.

## Stacking bonuses

| Bonus | Extra rolls | Since |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shiny Charm | +2 | B2W2 |
| Masuda method (parents from different languages) | +4 (Gen 4), +5 (Gen 5+) | DPPt |
| Masuda + Shiny Charm (Gen 6+) | 1/512 | XY |

- Shiny Charm: complete the regional Pokédex (the requirement changes per game).
- Masuda: the language is the one of the Pokémon's origin game; a foreign Ditto does the job.

## Shiny lock

- Some Pokémon can never be shiny in the game's own encounter (the game locks the odds).
- Examples: Victini, Reshiram and Zekrom (BW), Xerneas and Yveltal (XY), Cosmog, Solgaleo, Lunala and the Tapus (SM),
  Zacian, Zamazenta and Eternatus (SwSh), Koraidon, Miraidon and the legendary Paradox Pokémon (SV).
- Starters and gifts are usually locked since Gen 5 (unless the game says otherwise).
- Some are released later through events or in another game (e.g. shiny Zacian and Zamazenta by event).

## Tips

- Use the Hunt Counter to log encounters and eggs.
- Shiny locks and base odds: check the game's page before starting.
- A transferred shiny stays shiny (since Gen 3; also from Gen 2 to Gen 7 through Poké Transporter).
