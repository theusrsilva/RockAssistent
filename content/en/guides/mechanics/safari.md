## Common rules

- Pay the entry fee and get Safari Balls (or Park Balls); your Pokémon do not battle.
- Instead of attacking: bait (less likely to flee, harder to catch) or rock/mud (the opposite).
- The visit ends when the balls run out or the steps are used up.

## Bug-Catching Contest (GSC, HGSS)

- National Park: Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays (HGSS: more days in the postgame).
- 20 minutes, 20 Park Balls (GSC) / Sport Balls (HGSS); you only bring your first party Pokémon.
- The bug with the most points wins (level, IVs, remaining HP and rarity).
- Prizes: 1st Sun Stone (GSC), 2nd Everstone, 3rd Gold Berry; HGSS rolls stones and items.
- Rare: Scyther and Pinsir; in HGSS the list changes by day.
