## General rules

- Since Gen 2 each Pokémon can hold one item.
- Wild Pokémon may hold items (usually 50% / 5% for common/rare items; Compound Eyes or Super Luck in the lead
  raise this to 60% / 20%).
- Thief and Covet steal the item; against wild Pokémon you keep it.
- Items consumed in battle (Berries, Focus Sash) are used up; Harvest and Recycle bring them back.

## Where to find them (in general)

- Battle Point shops: Battle Frontier (Emerald, Pt, HGSS), Battle Subway (BW), Battle Maison (XY, ORAS),
  Battle Tree (SM, USUM), Battle Tower (SwSh).
- Delibird Presents (SV, Mesagoza and other cities) sells many battle items.
- SwSh: BP shops in Wyndon and Hammerlocke; Wild Area Watt Traders.
- Pickup (a Pokémon with the Ability in the party) and items held by wild Pokémon (Thief).
- Unique items (Leftovers, Choice, Life Orb) are often hidden: see each game's guide.
