## What it is

- Introduced in Gen 3. There are 25 natures.
- Each one raises one stat by 10% and lowers another by 10%. Five are neutral (they raise and lower the same stat).
- HP is never affected by nature.
- Gen 3–4: the nature comes from the Pokémon's PID (personality value).

- Flavored healing Berries (Figy, Wiki, Mago, Aguav, Iapapa) confuse a Pokémon that dislikes that flavor.
- In contests, a Pokéblock/Poffin of the liked flavor gives more condition.

## Picking a nature

- Physical attacker: Adamant or Jolly. Special: Modest or Timid.
- Lower the stat the Pokémon does not use (Sp. Atk on a physical one, Attack on a special one).
- Trick Room: Brave or Quiet (they lower Speed).

## How to control it

| Way | Games | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Synchronize in the lead | Emerald–Gen 7: 50%; SwSh: 100%; SV: no effect | Wild Pokémon get the lead's nature |
| Everstone on a parent | Emerald, Gen 4 and BW: 50% (DPPt: mother or Ditto only); B2W2+: 100% | The baby inherits the nature |
| Mints | SwSh and SV | Change the stat effect; the original nature stays recorded |
