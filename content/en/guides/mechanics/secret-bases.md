## Secret Bases (RSE)

- Requires TM43 Secret Power: a gift on Route 111 (near the desert entrance).
- Use Secret Power on: a hole in a rock wall (cave), a large bush or a tree vine.
- One base at a time; moving keeps the decorations.
- Decorations: Lilycove Department Store, Fortree City (decoration shop) and prizes.
- Pokémon dolls and cushions, desks, chairs, mats, posters.
- Record mixing (link cable) copies a friend's base into your game; you can battle the owner once a day.

## Secret Bases (ORAS)

- TM94 Secret Power: Aarune gives it on Route 111.
- Spots: trees, bushes, caves, more base types than RSE.
- Flags: take flags from other bases to raise your rank (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum).
- Sharing by StreetPass, internet and QR Code.
- Secret Pals: up to 5 characters who train with you and give buffs (healing, items etc.).

## Underground (DPPt)

- Explorer Kit: given by the Underground Man in Eterna City.
- Using the kit anywhere outdoors takes you underground.
- Digging: shining walls hold Spheres, Fossils, Evolution Stones, Plates, Heart Scales and more.
- Hammer and pickaxe: the wall cracks; stop before it collapses.
- Secret Base: the Digger Drill digs a base into a wall.
- Traps and flags of other players' bases (local multiplayer).
- Spheres trade for decorations and items with the underground vendors.

## Grand Underground (BDSP)

- Explorer Kit in Eterna City, like DPPt.
- Pokémon Hideaways: caves with wild Pokémon, including species from outside Sinnoh.
- Pokémon statues (dug up and placed in the base) change which types show up in the Hideaways.
- Secret Base with statues; Spheres to buy items.
- After the National Dex, more species and higher levels appear.
