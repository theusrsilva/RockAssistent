## Where they exist

| Games | Name | Place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RS | Pokémon Contests | Verdanturf (Normal), Fallarbor (Super), Slateport (Hyper), Lilycove (Master) |
| Emerald | Pokémon Contests | All ranks in Lilycove City |
| DPPt | Super Contests | Hearthome City |
| ORAS | Contest Spectaculars | Verdanturf, Fallarbor, Slateport and Lilycove |
| BDSP | Super Contest Shows | Hearthome City |

- RSE and ORAS: you need the Contest Pass (given at the first hall).

## Categories (conditions)

| Category | Flavor that raises it | Color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cool | Spicy | Red |
| Beauty | Dry | Blue |
| Cute | Sweet | Pink |
| Smart (Clever) | Bitter | Green |
| Tough | Sour | Yellow |

- Ranks: Normal → Super → Hyper → Master. Winning a rank unlocks the next one in that category.
- Each move has a category; a move matching the contest's category scores more.

## How each one works

- **RSE and ORAS:** appearance round (condition) and appeal round with 5 turns of moves; combos and crowd excitement.
- **DPPt:** three parts: Visual (dress-up with accessories), Dance (rhythm) and Acting (moves for the judges).
- **BDSP:** Visual and Dance (rhythm); no Acting part.

- Nature changes the gain: likes the flavor → more; dislikes → less (see [Natures](/guias/mechanics/natures/natures)).
- Feebas → Milotic: high Beauty and level up (Gen 3–4).

## Ribbons

- One ribbon per category and rank won.
- RSE: winning Master Rank puts the Pokémon's portrait in the Lilycove museum.
- ORAS: Lisia gives Cosplay Pikachu (5 costumes, each with an exclusive move) at the start of the contests.
- Hoenn and Sinnoh contest ribbons are different and stay on the Pokémon forever.
