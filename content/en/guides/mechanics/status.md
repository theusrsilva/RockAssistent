## Major status (stays after battle)

| Status | Effect | By generation | Immune |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burn (BRN) | Loses HP every turn and Attack is halved | Damage: 1/16 (Gen 1), 1/8 (Gen 2–6), 1/16 (Gen 7+) | Fire |
| Poison (PSN) | Loses HP every turn | 1/16 (Gen 1), 1/8 (Gen 2+) | Poison, Steel |
| Badly poisoned (TOX) | Damage grows: 1/16, 2/16, 3/16... | Gen 1–2: becomes regular poison on switching; Gen 3+: resets to 1/16 on switching | Poison, Steel |
| Paralysis (PAR) | 25% chance to not move; Speed drops | Speed ×0.25 (Gen 1–6), ×0.5 (Gen 7+) | Electric (Gen 6+); Ground vs Thunder Wave |
| Sleep (SLP) | Cannot move | 1–7 turns (Gen 1–2), 2–5 (Gen 3–4), 1–3 (Gen 5+) | Abilities like Insomnia |
| Freeze (FRZ) | Cannot move | Gen 1: only a Fire move thaws it; Gen 2+: 20% chance to thaw each turn (Gen 2: ~10%) | Ice (Gen 2+) |
| Frostbite | Replaces freeze in Legends: Arceus: 1/16 per turn and Sp. Atk halved | PLA only | Ice |
| Drowsy | Replaces sleep in Legends: Arceus: takes more damage and may lose its turn | PLA only | — |

- A Pokémon has only one major status at a time.
- Fire moves thaw a frozen target; some (Scald, Flame Wheel) thaw the user itself.

## Temporary status (gone on switching)

| Status | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Confusion | May hit itself (40-power hit): 50% (Gen 1–6), 33% (Gen 7+); lasts a few turns |
| Infatuation (Attract) | 50% chance to not move; opposite gender only |
| Flinch | Loses the turn; only if the move came first (Fake Out, King's Rock) |
| Leech Seed | Loses 1/8 HP per turn to the foe; Grass is immune |
| Trapped (Wrap, Bind, Fire Spin...) | Cannot switch and loses HP: 1/16 (Gen 2–5), 1/8 (Gen 6+) for 4–5 turns |
| Curse (Ghost) | Loses 1/4 HP per turn |
| Taunt | Damaging moves only |
| Encore | Repeats the last move |
| Perish Song | Faints in 3 turns unless it switches |
| Yawn | Falls asleep at the end of the next turn |

## Outside battle

- Poison on the map: Gen 1–4 loses HP while walking (Gen 3: can faint; Gen 4: stops at 1 HP and is cured). Gen 5+: no damage.
- Major status stays until cured (Pokémon Center, items, Berries).

## Useful Abilities

- Guts: +50% Attack with a status (ignores the burn cut).
- Poison Heal: heals while poisoned instead of losing HP.
- Synchronize: passes burn, poison and paralysis to whoever caused it.
- Early Bird: wakes up in half the time.
