## Mega Evolution

- Changes the form, the stats (+100 total) and sometimes the type and Ability.
- Lasts until the battle ends or the Pokémon faints. It cannot hold another item.
- New Speed applies from the next turn (Gen 6) or the same turn (Gen 7+).
- Mega Rayquaza: no stone; it must know Dragon Ascent (ORAS).
- XY: Mega Ring given in Shalour City (Tower of Mastery). ORAS: Mega Bracelet received in the story.
- Z-A: Mega Evolution returns; new Mega Stones and uncontrolled wild Megas (Rogue).

## Z-Moves

- A type's Z-Crystal turns a move of that type into a strong Z-Move (power based on the original move).
- Status Z-Move: the move works as usual + a bonus (stats, healing).
- Exclusive Z-Crystals: Pikanium Z (Pikachu), Decidium Z, Incinium Z, Primarium Z, Snorlium Z, Mimikium Z etc.
- Most come from trials and kahunas; Hala makes the Z-Ring from the Sparkling Stone.

## Dynamax and Gigantamax

- Dynamax: HP ×1.5 to ×2 (Dynamax Level 0–10), moves become Max Moves for 3 turns.
- Max Moves raise stats or set weather/terrain; Max Guard blocks everything.
- Immune to flinching, weight-based moves (Low Kick, Heavy Slam) and one-hit KO moves.
- Gigantamax: only species with the G-Max Factor; new form and an exclusive G-Max Move.
- Max Soup (Isle of Armor) gives or removes the G-Max Factor for some species.
- Dynamax Candy: raises the Dynamax Level.

## Terastal

- The Pokémon becomes its Tera Type: a single type for defense (weaknesses change).
- Keeps STAB from its original types and gains STAB from the Tera Type.
- Tera Type matching an original type: STAB ×2.
- Low-power moves of the Tera Type are raised to 60 power.
- Tera Blast becomes the Tera type (physical or special by the higher stat).
- Changing the Tera Type: 50 Tera Shards of the type, with the chef at the Medali restaurant.
- Stellar (The Indigo Disk DLC): ×1.2 on the first move of each type (×2 if it is an original type); Terapagos.

## Others

- Legends: Arceus: Agile Style and Strong Style (mastered moves), no once-per-battle gimmick.
- LGPE: Mega Evolution and partner-exclusive moves (Pikachu/Eevee).
