## IVs (genetics)

| Generations | Range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gen 1–2 | 0–15 (DVs) | The HP DV comes from the other four; in Gen 2 DVs also set shininess and gender |
| Gen 3+ | 0–31 per stat | Fixed from the encounter; add up to +31 to the stat at level 100 |

- Legendaries and mythicals (and Ultra Beasts in Gen 7+): at least 3 IVs of 31 since Gen 6; in XY it applies to the whole Undiscovered group.
- Breeding passes IVs from the parents (see [Breeding](/guias/mechanics/breeding/breeding)).

## EVs (training)

| Generations | Per-stat cap | Total cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gen 1–2 | 65,535 (Stat Exp.) | no total cap |
| Gen 3–5 | 255 | 510 |
| Gen 6+ | 252 | 510 |

- 4 EVs = 1 stat point at level 100. That is why the usual spread is 252/252/4.
- Each defeated species gives 1 to 3 EVs in a fixed stat (e.g. Geodude gives Defense).
- In Gen 1–2 any won battle gives Stat Exp. equal to the defeated Pokémon's base stats.

## Gaining EVs faster

| Item / method | Effect | Since |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vitamins (HP Up, Protein, Iron, Calcium, Zinc, Carbos) | +10 EVs; up to 100 per stat in Gen 3–7; no such cap in Gen 8+ | Gen 3 |
| Feathers / Wings | +1 EV | Gen 5 |
| Power items (Power Weight, Bracer, Belt, Lens, Band, Anklet) | +4 EVs per battle (Gen 4–6), +8 (Gen 7+) | Gen 4 |
| Macho Brace | Doubles EVs (cuts Speed) | Gen 3 |
| Pokérus | Doubles EVs | Gen 2 |
| Super Training | 3DS minigames | XY, ORAS |
| Poké Pelago (Isle Evelup) | Training while you play | SM, USUM |
| Poké Jobs | Jobs that give EVs | SwSh |
| Hordes / SOS / raids | Several defeated at once | XY, SM, SwSh, SV |

## Removing EVs

- Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa and Tamato Berries: Emerald: drops the stat to 100 if above it; Gen 4: same, then −10; Gen 5+: −10 per Berry. They also raise friendship.
- SV: Fresh-Start Mochi (The Teal Mask DLC, Ogre Oustin' prize) resets all EVs at once.

## Hyper Training

- Turns IVs into "Best" (31 for calculation only) with Bottle Caps.
- Bottle Cap = one stat; Gold Bottle Cap = all.
- Gen 7–8: Pokémon at level 100. SV: level 50 or higher.
- The original IV is still stored (it counts for breeding and Hidden Power).

## IV checker (Judge)

- Gen 4+ (Platinum, HGSS): an NPC rates the IVs with phrases; Emerald has a forerunner at the Battle Frontier.
- Gen 7+: Judge function in the PC, unlocked in the postgame (Battle Tree, Battle Tower or equivalent).
- The scale goes from "No Good" (0) to "Best" (31).

## Games with different systems

- LGPE: AVs (Awakening Values) from 0 to 200 per stat, gained with Candies.
- Legends: Arceus: Effort Levels (0–10) with Grit items.
