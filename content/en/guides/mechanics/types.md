## Multipliers

| Result | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Super effective | ×2 |
| Normal | ×1 |
| Not very effective | ×0.5 |
| No effect | ×0 |

- Dual-type Pokémon: the multipliers stack (×4, ×0.25).
- Move of the user's own type (STAB): ×1.5.

## By generation

- Gen 1: 15 types.
- Gen 2: Dark and Steel added (17 types).
- Gen 4: physical or special depends on the move, not its type.
- Gen 6: Fairy added (18 types).

Full chart: [Pokédex → Types](/pokedex/tipos).
