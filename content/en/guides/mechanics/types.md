| Not very effective | ×0.5 |

- Two types: the multipliers stack (×4, ×0.25).
- Move of the same type as the Pokémon (STAB): ×1.5 (×2 with Adaptability).

## Status immunities by type

- Fire: cannot be burned. Ice: cannot be frozen. Poison and Steel: cannot be poisoned.
- Electric: cannot be paralyzed (Gen 6+). Grass: immune to powders (Spore, Sleep Powder; Gen 6+).
- Ghost: cannot be trapped (Mean Look, Arena Trap; Gen 6+). Dark: immune to Prankster moves (Gen 7+).

## Changes by generation

- Gen 1: 15 types. Ghost did not affect Psychic (a bug: it was meant to be super effective). Bug and Poison were super effective against each other. Ice was neutral against Fire.
- Gen 2: Dark and Steel are added (17 types). Ghost becomes ×2 on Psychic; Poison becomes neutral against Bug; Bug becomes not very effective on Poison; Fire resists Ice.
- Gen 1–3: physical or special depended on the type (physical: Normal, Fighting, Flying, Poison, Ground, Rock, Bug, Ghost, Steel).
- Gen 4: physical or special now depends on the move.
- Gen 6: Fairy is added (18 types). Steel no longer resists Ghost and Dark.
- SV: Terastal changes the defensive type; the Stellar type only attacks (see [Battle gimmicks](/guias/mechanics/battle-gimmicks/battle-gimmicks)).
