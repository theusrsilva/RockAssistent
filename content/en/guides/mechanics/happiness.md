## What it is

- Hidden value from 0 to 255 (friendship or happiness). Exists since Gen 2 (Yellow's Pikachu already had something similar).
- Friendship evolutions: 220+ in Gen 2–7; 160+ in Gen 8+.
- Return gets stronger with high friendship; Frustration with low friendship (up to 102 power).

## Starting value

| Case | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Caught (most species, up to Gen 7) | 70 |
| Caught (most species, Gen 8+) | 50 |
| Hatched | 120 |
| Friend Ball | 200 (Gen 2–7), 150 (Gen 8+) |
| Some legendaries and hard species | 0 or 35 |

## What raises it

| Action | Approx. gain (Gen 3–7) |
| --- | --- |
| Level up | +5 / +3 / +2 (by current friendship: <100 / <200 / 200+) |
| Walking (every 128 steps) | +1 (the chance varies by game) |
| Vitamins | +5 / +3 / +2 |
| EV-lowering Berries (Pomeg etc.) | +10 / +5 / +2 |
| Wing/Feather | +3 / +2 / +1 |
| Massage or haircut | up to +30 (once a day, varies by game) |
| Gym Leader/Elite Four/Champion battle | +3 / +2 / +1 |

- Held Soothe Bell: bigger gains (about ×1.5).
- Luxury Ball: a Pokémon caught in it gains friendship faster.
- Gen 6+: Pokémon-Amie (XY, ORAS), Pokémon Refresh (SM), camping (SwSh) and picnics (SV) also raise it.

## What lowers it

| Action | Approx. loss |
| --- | --- |
| Fainting | −1 (−5 to −10 if the foe is 30+ levels higher) |
| Energy Powder / Heal Powder | −5 / −10 |
| Energy Root | −10 / −15 |
| Revival Herb | −15 / −20 |

## How to check

- NPCs who rate the bond: Goldenrod City (GSC, HGSS), Verdanturf Town (RSE), Hearthome City (DPPt, BDSP).
- SwSh and SV: the Pokémon walking with you shows its reaction; in SV an NPC also rates it.
- Return: 102 power = friendship 255.

## Affection (Gen 6–7)

- Pokémon-Amie/Refresh has a separate value, affection: with high affection the Pokémon dodges, endures a fatal hit
  and gains more experience.
- In Gen 8 affection was merged into friendship.
