## Egg Groups

- Two Pokémon breed if they are male and female and share an Egg Group.
- 15 groups: Monster, Water 1, Water 2, Water 3, Bug, Flying, Field, Fairy, Grass, Human-Like, Mineral, Amorphous, Dragon, Ditto, Undiscovered.
- Ditto breeds with anything except Ditto and Undiscovered. It is the only way for genderless ones (Magnemite, Bronzor...).
- Undiscovered cannot breed: legendaries, most mythicals and babies (Pichu, Togepi...).
- The baby is the most basic form of the mother's species (or of the non-Ditto parent).
- Incense babies: Wynaut, Munchlax, Mantyke etc. only hatch if a parent holds the incense (Gen 3–8; SV drops this).

## What is inherited

| What | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| IVs | Gen 3+: 3 IVs come from the parents; Destiny Knot (Gen 6+): 5 IVs |
| Power items | Gen 4+: guarantee that the item's stat comes from that parent |
| Nature | Everstone: 50% (Emerald, Gen 4, BW; DPPt mother or Ditto only), 100% (B2W2+) |
| Hidden Ability | Gen 5: mother only; Gen 6+: also a male with Ditto (60%) |
| Poké Ball | Gen 6: mother; Gen 7+: either parent of the same species (Ditto does not pass it) |
| Egg Moves | Gen 2–5: from the father; Gen 6+: from either parent |
| Level-up moves | Moves the baby would learn that both parents know |
| Regional form | The parent holding an Everstone sets the form (Gen 7+) |

## Egg Moves without breeding

- SwSh: in the Nursery, Egg Moves pass between two of the same species.
- SV: holding a Mirror Herb at a picnic, the Pokémon copies Egg Moves from another party member that knows them.

## Hatching

- Eggs are counted in cycles; each cycle is a fixed number of steps (256 in most games).
- Flame Body, Magma Armor or Steam Engine in the party: half the steps (since Emerald; Steam Engine since SwSh).
- Oval Charm (Gen 5+): eggs appear more often.
- Babies hatch at level 1 (Gen 4+) or 5 (Gen 2–3).
- Starting friendship 120 and the OT of whoever hatched it.
- Riding the bike in a straight line with a Flame Body Pokémon is the fastest way.

## Shiny from eggs

- Masuda method: parents from different languages (Gen 4+). Details in [Shiny](/guias/mechanics/shiny/shiny).
- Gen 2: a shiny parent raises the baby's odds.
